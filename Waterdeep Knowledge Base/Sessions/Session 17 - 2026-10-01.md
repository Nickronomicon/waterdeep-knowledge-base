# Session 17 - 2026-10-01

The Skullduggers left the Watch-wagon ambush behind, learned that Zardoz Zord sold all eight known Luskan-design nimblewrights in Waterdeep, and heard the Cassalanters' plea to save their children with Neverember's hidden gold. A brief investigation ended with Vincent Trench refunding his fee and refusing to cross Menzoberranzan. The party's next proposed score is Zord's buyer names.

## Start And End State

- **Start:** Ches 22, 1492 DR, after Agorn Fuoco escaped in the Watch wagon with Urstul Floxin. Two Watch guards and both thugs are dead; the dead spellcaster's body went away on the wagon.
- **End:** That evening/night, upstairs at [[Places/Trollskull Manor]], after Trench's warning and departure. No day advance or long rest is shown.
- **Party:** All five PCs present. Elior, Jonathan, and Broethuul visited Cassalanter Villa; Aladeen and Davy stayed at Trollskull. The villa visitors shared the full account afterward.

## Major Scenes

### Heist Briefing Before Play

The DM explained five phases: identify the score, gather information, onsite surveillance, preparation, and operation. Research and surveillance can attract attention. A Foundry planning scene will collect discovered floorplans and security information. Each PC declares one skill, tool, attribute, or ability for prep, then describes and rolls that preparation when an obstacle makes it relevant during the operation. This was a table-procedure briefing; nobody declared a prep skill or began an operation. See [[Reference/Remix Eye Heists]].

### Ambush Cleanup And Departure

- The two dead thugs had black snake tattoos, crude maces, one working heavy crossbow, and one crushed crossbow. The dead mage was on the departing wagon and could not be searched.
- The thugs carried 18 sp and 3 cp; the two guards carried another 5 sp and 5 cp. The looter took **23 sp and 8 cp total**, openly acknowledging the money to the party. The diarization does not securely identify the looter.
- The guards' distinctive Watch uniforms were left. Other weapons were described as available; taking them into inventory was not confirmed.
- A Sleight of Hand result of 15 left the searched bodies looking reasonably undisturbed. The party chose to leave rather than wait for or seek the Watch.
- Group Stealth results were 13, 9, 11, 18, and 3; the DM judged the departure successfully concealed as far as the party could tell. Broethuul staged a thug's mace in the damaged flower cart to disguise his own part in wrecking it.
- Sending a report to an ally was suggested, but no such message was actually sent on screen.

### Remallia's Raven And The Zord Lead

At Trollskull, cordons and heavy Watch presence remained. Bodies and serious casualties had been removed; Saeth Cromley continued examining the scene, while Barnibus Blastwind had left. Fala's body and Tally were absent; the DM suggested Tally was probably with the remains, without confirming their location.

[[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree|Remallia's]] silver raven dropped a replacement [[Objects/Remallia's Paper Bird|paper bird]] for Aladeen and delivered her recorded message: only eight known city nimblewrights match Luskan construction, and all eight were sold by [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]]. She had no buyer names and warned that openly asking could show the seller what the party knows.

The party discussed using Elior's status to pose as a prospective buyer seeking customer references for a surprise parental gift. They also proposed finding [[People/NPCs/Margo Verida]], Zord's opera companion, to learn how to approach him. Neither plan was executed. Existing owner leads—[[Places/House of Inspired Hands]], [[People/NPCs/Bojentra Summertaen]], and [[People/NPCs/Davin Crommor]]—were reiterated.

### Hiring Vincent Trench

The party visited [[Places/Tiger's Eye]] and gave [[People/NPCs/Vincent Trench]] their Fireball/Luskan-nimblewright findings. They hired him to investigate Zord and the nimblewright sales, choosing to locate Margo themselves.

- Fee: 50 gp upfront; another 50 gp if work extends beyond a tenday; half refunded if he finds nothing.
- Each PC contributed 10 gp. Results were to be delivered to the manor, with private upstairs conversation available.
- Davy disclosed to the whole party that he took Dalakhar's gemstone pouch. He still held it; no sale, appraisal, or payment in gems occurred.

### Cassalanter Summons And Plea

[[People/NPCs/Willifort Crowelle]] arrived at Trollskull requesting immediate attendance at [[Places/Cassalanter Villa]]. The three visitors received unusually courteous treatment from the gate guards. Broethuul played a feather-tickling game with [[People/NPCs/Terenzio Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Elzerina Cassalanter]] before Ammalia and an elderly tiefling nanny called Nana intervened. No personal name beyond Nana was established for the nanny.

[[People/NPCs/Ammalia Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Victoro Cassalanter]] met them in a reading room overlooking the garden, with a mounted black dragon head. They said the Fireball damage did not threaten their confidence in the tavern investment. Their actual request concerned the twins. These are **Cassalanter claims**, not independently verified history:

- An Asmodean cultist cursed the twins at birth, nearly nine years ago, then killed himself as part of the rite. Asmodeus will take their souls on their ninth birthday, roughly twenty days away according to the table explanation.
- Clerics confirmed the curse but could not remove it. Family agents recently found an old Asmodean text suggesting a ritual exchange: melt/burn **999,999 gold coins** in place of the children's souls. Victoro acknowledged that the text might be wrong.
- Even liquidating their holdings would leave them short. Neverember's Enigma is **500,000 embezzled dragons** hidden in Waterdeep; recovering it would supply enough.
- Their agents followed [[People/NPCs/Dalakhar]] because they believed he held the [[Objects/Stone of Golorr]], the key to the gold. They described a palm-sized rounded artifact with three eyes that stores knowledge. Broethuul and Elior found the name familiar but recalled no further lore.
- They offered **loan forgiveness and 10% of the recovered hoard** (50,000 gp if the full stated hoard is recovered). Victoro said refusal would leave the existing business arrangement intact.

The visitors promised a party discussion and an answer **by the following morning**, without immediately accepting for the absent PCs. Elior questioned the curse, clerical evidence, ritual mechanics, and household agents. Willifort claimed to have witnessed the ritual, suggested jealous rivals as a motive, and linked possible resentment to the household's rise. He said he trusted the text's provenance but could not guarantee its truth. No agents or clerics were actually interviewed, and no text was inspected. See [[Objects/Cassalanter Rescue Offer]].

### Tavern Recovery, Margo, And Lif

- Trollskull was semi-open, feeding and serving subdued neighbors after triage. Glass had been cleared, but the windows were still missing. The cooks had been working throughout the day; free relief provisions were possible, with no exact cost recorded.
- Aladeen asked patrons about Margo. They knew the famous local singer and placed sightings in Castle Ward, particularly [[Places/Smiling Siren]], a nightclub/theater where she sometimes performs spontaneously. Her home address remained unknown.
- Lif's chalkboard was restored with a condolence message and fresh chalk, but he did not respond. Patric Nivka had informally taken over drink service in Lif's absence.
- Davy considered a homemade seance. A Religion result of 14 supplied ways to attract a spirit's attention, not power to compel contact.
- Davy checked [[Places/Corellon's Crown]] and the alley for Fala's spirit. Fresh timbers shored the damaged corner, probably Tally's or his apprentices' work; the interior was otherwise largely tidy and deserted. No obvious haunting appeared. Davy lacks the spiritual expertise to establish whether Fala remains; Dorgar would be needed for deeper inquiry.
- Davy promised Lif to try to reunite him with Fala **if Fala is still around**. No response followed.
- Short rests were allowed for everyone during the downtime. Davy changed his borrowed proficiency to the healer's kit; exact final HP and other resource totals were not recorded. Song of Rest was discussed for Aladeen and Davy, not established for the villa visitors.

### Party Decision And Trench's Warning

Back upstairs, the visitors shared the Cassalanter account. The group connected it to the tube, the gnome, the Zhentarim, and the Fireball. The DM reminded them that witnesses saw the nimblewright search Dalakhar: its owner is a likely Stone lead, while Urstul's possession of the Stone remains unproven.

The party reached an **internal agreement to pursue the gold and agree to help**, with Davy qualifying his support: the children are innocent, but he distrusts the Cassalanters and wants the gold secured before deciding its disposition. A final reply to the family remains unsent.

Trench arrived at the upstairs door the same night, returned the **full 50 gp**, and refused the job. He recommended dropping Zord, said the party's names had never come up, and asked that his own involvement be denied. After a Persuasion result of 19, he disclosed only that he would not get entangled with **[[Places/Menzoberranzan]]**. Most PCs recognized the Underdark drow city; Jonathan failed the recall check. The DM confirmed a dark-elf connection but explicitly cautioned that this does not establish Lolth's direct involvement.

The proposed first heist is acquiring Zord's nimblewright buyer names. No buyer list, storage location, ship layout, or security plan was obtained.

## Uncertainties And Reveal Boundaries

- **Deadline:** dialogue places the birthday a week after Founder's Feast, calls the feast about a tenday away, and rounds the total to roughly twenty days. Prep instead names Tarsahk 11 for the birthday and Tarsahk 9 for a ritual deadline. Those absolute dates and the earlier ritual cutoff were not revealed in play; confirm the campaign calendar before fixing a countdown date.
- **Clerical account:** Victoro names an Ilmater cleric first; Willifort calls a Dawnfather cleric the first to confirm the curse. Preserve this discrepancy rather than invent a reconciliation.
- **Artifact:** description and alleged purpose are party-known; actual possession, operation, missing Eyes, and faction Eye holdings remain unconfirmed or unrevealed.
- **Drow:** Trench's warning establishes a Menzoberranzan connection, not Zord's true identity, the Bregan D'aerthe name, a particular house, or Lolth's orders.
- **Speaker identity:** speaker labels collapse much of the conversation into one label. Scene context supports the villa split; individual looter attribution and some incidental remarks remain uncertain.
- **Fala:** absence of an obvious haunting is not proof that no spirit exists. The remains' destination and Tally's whereabouts were inferred, not observed.
- **Prep unused:** no docks visit, Fergus scene, Zord meeting, Gralhund reveal, Cassalanter Eye reveal, scar reaction, heist prep declaration, or operation occurred.
