# Session 18 - 2026-10-15

The Skullduggers accepted the Cassalanters' request, found Margo at the Smiling Siren, and defeated a Xanathar capture team during her rehearsal. Her introduction secured an audience with Zardoz Zord, but threats closed the conversation without buyer names. Elior spotted a likely customer ledger on Zord's desk, turning the investigation into a proposed Eyecatcher heist.

## Start And End State

- **Start:** Early morning Ches 23, 1492 DR, at [[Places/Trollskull Manor]]. All five PCs received an explicitly confirmed long rest after Session 17.
- **End:** Later the same day at/returning to the [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair]] pier in the Dock Ward. Aladeen and Elior have been rowed back from the Eyecatcher; Davy, Jonathan, and Broethuul have finished dockside scouting. No evening, further rest, or date advance was established.
- **Next declared direction:** Speak with [[People/NPCs/Zelifarn]] and continue research/surveillance for a ledger operation. No dragon visit, formal individual prep declarations, or heist execution occurred.

## Major Scenes

### Cassalanter Answer And Calendar

The opening recap explicitly named **Tarsahk 11** as the twins' ninth birthday and **Tarsahk 9** as the family's planned gold-sacrifice ritual. The earlier rough twenty-day estimate is superseded by those named dates, though the DM's spoken eighteen/nineteen-day arithmetic was approximate. Keep the family's origin/cure story unverified and its Ilmater-versus-Dawnfather account unresolved; see [[Objects/Cassalanter Rescue Offer]].

The party penned and dispatched its promised acceptance, agreeing to help and keeping the family fairly up to date on the nimblewright/Stone investigation. Davy's earlier reservation about the hoard's final disposition was not withdrawn. The 2,000 gp loan is still active; conditional forgiveness and the 10% reward have not been earned.

### Possible Followers And Margo's Rehearsal

Davy noticed repeated faces on the walk through the market and warned the group of possible surveillance, without identifying a follower or faction. Broethuul's conspicuous look around and feather-tickling of a passerby supplied no reliable identification.

The [[Places/Smiling Siren]] is an older, well-maintained neighborhood theater on **Siren Lane, off Tharlion Street**, near the Castle Ward market. The party found [[People/NPCs/Margo Verida]] rehearsing with a pianist while stagehands rigged a painted sea-serpent/dragon prop. At Elior's request she sent the workers and accompanist away for privacy.

The party explained the Fireball and Zord's connection through nimblewright sales; it did not explicitly identify its Harper source. Margo said she was staying aboard Zord's ship as his guest, with room and board during his festival-season stay. She described him as a longtime fan, loud and public but protective of his personal space. A worthwhile story could interest him. She doubted his involvement in the bombing and offered a letter of introduction, asking to be left out of any nefarious scheme. She had her own performance that night and did not accompany the party.

### Xanathar Capture Team

Before the introduction was delivered, a duergar and gazer approached. The duergar referred to the party's inquiries about nimblewrights and dead gnomes and demanded they come speak to his employer. A second duergar was initially invisible. Margo fled backstage; both duergar and the gazer died in the fight.

- Aladeen mocked the gazer, supported allies with inspiration, shot enemies using Lucky rerolls, and healed Davy for 8 HP.
- Elior tried to Thunderwave the enlarged duergar beneath the suspended dragon prop. The enemy saved and stayed put; **Jonathan and Broethuul each took 9 thunder damage and were pushed 10 feet**. The prop was never dropped. Elior later healed Jonathan for 15 HP.
- The invisible duergar struck Davy for 9 damage before becoming visible. Davy later critically stabbed him, disengaged, and ultimately shot down the gazer.
- Jonathan killed the enlarged duergar with a critical morningstar/Divine Smite for **36 damage**, announcing that justice had been served in an unusual gravelly voice.
- Broethuul used Rite of the Equinox, killed the second duergar with his rapier, then fell unconscious to the gazer's fear/cold rays. Jonathan restored him with **9 HP** before any death save was shown. No subsequent rest was recorded; exact final HP/resources are not reconstructed.
- No enemy survived for interrogation. Margo returned shaken and explicitly identified the trouble as Xanathar-linked.

### Letter, Loot, And Limited Watch Report

Margo handed over [[Objects/Margo's Letter of Introduction]]; Broethuul accepted it and gave her his purple feather. She retired to her dressing room. The party looted the duergar and found no cash:

| Loot | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Standard healing potion | One, 2d4 + 2; Elior handled inventory entry. Not consumed on screen. |
| Compact climbing rig | Folding grappling hook, 50 feet silk rope, two pitons in a felt-lined roll; ordinary rope/grapple function, concealable hook. Elior handled item entry; final distribution otherwise uncertain. |
| [[Objects/Tap Stones]] | One common, unattuned pair with matching carved eye marks; Elior recorded them. |
| [[Objects/Xanathar Capture Orders]] | Short note ordering one Trollskull member brought in alive for information about the gnome and Stone; exact carrier not secure. |

The orders led the party to infer that Xanathar was still seeking the Stone and probably did not own the attacking nimblewright. This is an inference, not independent proof of custody or ownership.

The party informed **two Watch patrolmen** of a commotion at the theater while leaving, avoiding a detailed interview. The patrol headed that way without urgency. No formal statement, arrest, or official finding followed on screen. This report does not resolve the unreported Session 16/17 wagon ambush.

### Dockside Fair And Split Investigation

The group discussed stealth, invisibility, diversions, disguises, another investigator, and dragon help, but committed to a social visit plus dockside reconnaissance first. **Aladeen and Elior** used the letter; **Davy, Jonathan, and Broethuul** scouted the fair.

Two nearly identical ships sit across a pier amid stalls, jugglers, sword swallowers, fire breathers, and a beer garden that opens later. The flagship **Eyecatcher remains about a mile offshore**. The introductory ship naming briefly contradicted this; see Uncertainties.

- [[People/NPCs/Klarr Besham]], the serious Heartbreaker captain with a shoulder tarantula, read Margo's letter, returned it, and rowed out to request an audience. He came back to escort the two visitors.
- [[People/NPCs/Fergus Crabwater]], the jovial Hellraiser captain, offered wine while they waited. Aladeen pretended to keep pace using Sleight of Hand; **Elior became mildly intoxicated**, receiving disadvantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma checks under the table ruling. No later sobriety recovery was recorded.
- Klarr led them across Eyecatcher's main deck to the **aftcastle captain's cabin**, telling them to stay close and not wander. He waited outside.

### Zord Refuses; A Likely Ledger Emerges

[[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]] welcomed Margo's friends, recognized them as Renaer's rescuers, and heard their Fireball account and culprit description. They deliberately omitted Dalakhar and the Stone. Zord invoked customer discretion and refused to release records to unofficial investigators. Elior alleged Watch corruption; this was a PC assertion, not a proven new fact.

Elior threatened reputational harm, rolling a natural 20 for a total Intimidation of 23. Zord took the threat seriously without being cowed, emphasized that his word carried weight offshore, and ended the meeting. He told Klarr to escort them ashore and to tell Margo he wanted a word. No assault, theft, deal, buyer name, or Gralhund reveal occurred.

On reflection, **Elior noticed Zord repeatedly glance at a particular bound book on his desk whenever customers or nimblewrights were mentioned**. The DM put his confidence at roughly 70% that it was the ledger or a related record. [[Objects/Zord's Suspected Customer Ledger]] is a concrete score and location lead, not a read or verified buyer list.

### Reconnaissance And Heist Discussion

- Davy watched launches shuttle between the docked ships and Eyecatcher. Two additional launches came and went during the social visit. Supply traffic suggested similar layouts/cargo-hold positions on the docked ships; no interior map was acquired.
- Eyecatcher's crew visibly became alert when yacht-sized or larger vessels approached. Small skiffs/rowboats did not provoke the same visible reaction; this does not prove they go undetected or unchallenged.
- Aladeen/Elior saw roughly **16-20 crew** aboard; the DM estimated **30-40** as a likely full complement. Davy counted roughly **20-30 carnival performers**, with sailors mostly staying aboard; these are rough counts, not a manifest.
- Jonathan saw heavy Watch presence and no overt attack, but could not tell whether the security level was unusual for the fair.
- Broethuul's employment inquiry failed: the troupe was insular, knew one another, and appeared to employ no locals. No job or infiltration cover was secured. The DM agreed to account for his deliberate gazer lookout through an unspecified passive-perception bonus; no new watcher was identified.
- The party wants buyer information and would prefer to read/copy it without leaving a missing book. Whether to steal the ledger if necessary remains undecided.
- Zelifarn as transport or diversion, hired/rented/stolen launches, smoke, fake faction identities, glamours, and assistance from Harpers/Cassalanters were proposals only. No purchases, spellcasting for infiltration, dragon agreement, or formal prep selections occurred.
- Davy confirmed a **forgery kit**, correcting the DM's suggestion of a disguise kit.
