# Session 14 - 2026-08-06

Session 14 resolves the opening-night fallout at [[Places/Trollskull Manor]], follows the broken [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]] to [[Places/Yellowspire]], rescues [[People/NPCs/Nat]] from a Banite Zhentarim cell, and ends on the mid-morning Ches 22 Fireball cliffhanger before any investigation or casualty count is revealed.

## Starting State

- The session began around midnight at the end of Ches 19, 1492 DR, after Trollskull Manor's grand opening.
- The tavern had 637 gp left from the original 2,000 gp [[Objects/Cassalanter Loan]], and the first 40 gp/tenday interest period had begun because the tavern was open.
- [[People/NPCs/Oren Fallow]], the Zhentarim-adjacent runner, was upstairs and still under [[People/PCs/Aladeen|Aladeen's]] Suggestion to stand behind [[People/PCs/Jonathan Usman Sonny Theodore Icarus Charles Eagleshield|Jonathan]] until released.
- [[People/NPCs/Thugby]] was also upstairs after accidentally attending the opening.
- [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]], [[People/NPCs/Floon Blagmaar]], and [[People/NPCs/Laraelra Harsard|Elra Harsard]] remained downstairs as the last patrons.
- [[People/NPCs/Hustus Staget]] and the [[Factions/City Watch]] were on the way to investigate the bodies outside the tavern.

## The Tube Is Opened

The party dropped Aladeen's concentration on Oren, who immediately begged for the sealed message tube back and explained that he was paid to deliver sealed messages, not read them. He did not know the contents, but he knew the black flying-snake seal and the destination were enough to be terrified.

The party bought time by sending [[People/PCs/Davy]] to acquire a forgery kit from a late-night Market contact for 15 gp. Before Davy returned, the party opened the tube anyway while Oren panicked and plugged his ears to avoid hearing anything inside.

The revealed message established:

- "The gnome" was alive.
- He had changed carts after the yard and was still asking after old Neverember channels or a similar phrase; the transcript hears this unclearly.
- He had "the thing" or knew where it slept.
- He kept saying the Stone remembered the road but not the eyes.
- Xanathar's people were on the same trail and had a floating watcher near the north-side alleys.
- If the lord's son had found a new house, the recipient should watch the house.
- The message should not be brought to the Portal because there were too many ears.

Elior tried handwriting analysis and concluded the writer had rough, utilitarian penmanship, likely not noble and probably used to more practical violence than fine letters. The table considered the "lord's son" likely to mean Renaer Neverember. The addressee `D.` and signer `Y.` remain unresolved at the table.

When Davy returned, he attempted to reseal the tube with the forgery kit and Aladeen's Bardic Inspiration. The reseal was passable at a glance, but a small corner of paper stuck out from under the cap.

The party then returned the tube to Oren after he revealed its destination was Yellowspire in the Castle Ward. Oren said the tube had been handed to him in the Trade Ward before Xanathar's people chased him. He snatched the tube and ran toward the Castle Ward.

## Watch Response

Staget arrived with six Watch members, recognized Elior, noted the same faction tattoos from the Candle Lane warehouse case, and questioned whether the party was doing vigilante work. Elior's legal/self-defense explanation was not especially persuasive, and Staget entered the tavern to question remaining witnesses.

Elra intervened quietly, speaking with Staget and helping keep the Watch response from becoming a worse immediate problem. Renaer and Floon left after being shown the copied or remembered message, with Renaer visibly uneasy about any mention of old Neverember business. He reasoned that "the Portal" likely meant the Yawning Portal and that someone only gives that warning if the recipient frequents it.

Aladeen played "Closing Time" at the remaining patrons and gave Staget an evasive statement. Staget made clear he was not trying to punish a tavern for defending itself, but he needed enough truth for paperwork and did not want to keep returning to Trollskull for bodies.

## Thugby

Aladeen questioned Thugby over a good drink. Thugby insisted he had mostly stayed out of trouble and knew nothing about the current runner/tube clash.

His only fresh lead was that he had recently been hired to guard a carriage moving daily from a North Ward villa on Vondel Street, between High Road and Copper Street, to Piergeiron's Palace in the Castle Ward and back. Jonathan identified the villa as House Cragspear's residence. Thugby did not know who rode in the carriage, and he left as soon as he safely could.

## Ches 20 And Nat's Kidnapping

On Ches 20, the first normal morning after opening began with [[People/NPCs/Lillian Haekin]] setting up breakfast and morning tea. Tally delivered the three wooden training bucklers Aladeen had commissioned for Nat, [[People/NPCs/Jenks]], and [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]].

The urchins were gone most of the day. Near sunset, Jenks and Squiddly burst into Trollskull filthy, frantic, and talking over each other. They reported that Nat had been grabbed just up from the alley about five minutes earlier. They had been following a black flying snake because it looked like the bad mark, then men with flying-snake tattoos knocked Jenks and Squiddly down and took Nat.

A black flying snake dropped [[Objects/Yellowspire Demand Note]]. The note named the runner as Oren Fallow and demanded Oren, the tube, the message, and every copy at Yellowspire by moonrise on Ches 21, with a warning not to tell the Watch.

## Finding Oren

The party sought help from Blackstaff Tower first. Elra attempted to scry Oren using his name, description, and Davy's rough sketch, but the second-hand information produced multiple vague images and no reliable location. Elra promised to keep looking and send word if she learned anything.

The party then used [[Objects/Remallia's Paper Bird]] to contact [[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree]] and ask for Harper help. While they continued to the [[Places/Yawning Portal]], Remallia's silver raven replied with Oren's likely room: [[Places/The Sleeping Wench]] on Snail Street in the Dock Ward.

At the Yawning Portal, the party asked around. [[People/NPCs/Yagra Stonefist]] reacted badly to people asking about Zhentarim business. [[People/NPCs/Bonnie]] helped Davy narrow the lead to Oren's drinking companions around Snail Street.

At the Sleeping Wench, Aladeen learned from a table of Zhent-marked drinkers that Oren was upstairs. Jonathan kicked open the right door and found Oren in a half-packed room with the badly resealed tube on the bed. Oren tried to dive out the window with the tube, but Jonathan grabbed him by the ankle. Aladeen's second Suggestion landed, forcing Oren to smile and follow Jonathan by no more than five feet for eight hours.

The party brought Oren and the tube back to Trollskull, tied him up, rested, and planned for the moonrise exchange.

## Yellowspire

At moonrise on Ches 21, the party brought Oren, the tube, and the original message to [[Places/Yellowspire]]. Elior carried the tube. They did not have physical copies, though the message had been remembered and repeated.

Yellowspire proved to be a yellowed old tower in the Castle Ward. The inside showed black hand symbols of Bane, one of the Dead Three, marking it as a Banite stronghold tied to the Zhentarim pressure. The party was escorted to [[People/NPCs/Amath Sercent]], a bald Mulan priestess in rich but simple robes.

Oren tried to explain that Xanathar's people had chased him, the party had opened the message, and he had been magically compelled and later tied up. The party contested parts of his story, and Amath accepted enough of their framing to quiet him. She then decided she had Oren, the tube, and the message, and ordered her acolytes to kill the party.

Combat followed:

- Broethuul opened Rite of the Storm but missed Amath with his first rapier strike.
- Aladeen shot Amath with a crossbow bolt and inspired Elior.
- Jonathan missed Amath and cast Shield of Faith.
- An acolyte cast a pale green-black flame effect at Jonathan, who avoided it.
- Elior used Innate Sorcery, metamagic, and a second-level Ice Knife to badly injure Amath.
- Another acolyte hit Jonathan with poison magic.
- Amath cast Hold Person, paralyzing Elior, then fled downstairs while ordering the others to kill the party and saying she would report to Manshoon.
- A smoke-wreathed, red-eyed infernal panther joined the fight.
- Broethuul killed one acolyte.
- Davy killed another acolyte with a hidden crossbow shot.
- The panther mauled the paralyzed Elior before Aladeen healed him.
- Davy later killed the panther with a crossbow shot.

Oren broke free of his ropes during the fight and fled without the tube. Broethuul, Jonathan, and the panther all struck at him as he ran, and the party shot him several times as he escaped, but Oren survived and disappeared into the city.

## Amath's Escape And Nat's Rescue

After the lower-floor fight, the party found a locked basement room. Jonathan forced the door open. Inside was a permanent teleportation circle, confirming Amath's escape route and suggesting Yellowspire connects to frequently used Zhentarim/Manshoon infrastructure.

Broethuul went upstairs and found Nat bound and gagged, held by another Banite acolyte with a knife. The acolyte tried to bargain for the scroll and an escape route. Broethuul refused to let her leave with Nat. Nat tried to elbow or struggle free but failed.

When the standoff broke down, the acolyte shouted praise to Bane, stabbed Nat for four points of damage, shoved her bleeding into Broethuul, and tried to rush past. Broethuul skewered the acolyte with his storm-lit rapier, killing her. Aladeen healed Nat back to full health. Nat, still bound, tried to hug the nearest rescuer and then had her hands freed so she could sign again.

The tower was left mostly empty except for acolyte bodies and the dead infernal panther. Amath escaped. Oren escaped. Elior kept the Zhentarim-stamped scroll tube.

## Return And Fireball

The party returned late to Trollskull with Nat. Jenks and Squiddly were overjoyed, Nat signed rapidly about what happened, and the kitchen staff were visibly relieved.

After a long rest, the party woke on Ches 22 with the sense that Trollskull had truly survived its opening: a gang attack, a hostage crisis, and an intact found family.

At mid-morning, before any Fireball investigation began, the room went white. The new windows shattered inward. The boom struck just after the light, throwing the party to the floor as a large detonation rattled Trollskull Alley.

The session ended there.

## Ending State

- The current in-world date is mid-morning on Ches 22, 1492 DR.
- Trollskull Manor is open and has suffered shattered windows from the explosion outside.
- Fireball has occurred as a sensory cliffhanger only. No cause, named dead, casualty count, nimblewright clue, Dalakhar reveal, Stone reveal, or Watch procedure has been established at the table yet.
- Nat is alive and back at Trollskull after being kidnapped, stabbed, healed, and rescued.
- Oren Fallow escaped after taking serious wounds. His relationship to Amath's cell, Davil/Yagra, and other Zhentarim actors remains unresolved.
- Amath Sercent escaped via a permanent teleportation circle and intended to report to Manshoon.
- Elior has the Zhentarim-stamped scroll tube and message.
- [[Objects/Remallia's Paper Bird]] has been used to contact Remallia.
- Thugby is no longer at Trollskull and appears to be a frightened red herring for the runner/tube conflict.
