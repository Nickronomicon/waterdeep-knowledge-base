# Session 16 - 2026-09-24

Session 16 rewound the end of [[Sessions/Session 15 - 2026-08-20]] so [[People/PCs/Aladeen]] could play through the Fireball investigation himself. The visit to [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair]] was explicitly retconned: the party did **not** go to the Dock Ward or speak to Captain Fergus after session 15. Instead, Aladeen pushed the group to call on [[People/NPCs/Vajra Safahr]] at [[Places/Blackstaff Tower]], contact [[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree]], and then stumble into a Zhentarim extraction of [[People/NPCs/Urstul Floxin]].

## Retcon Outcome

- The stable Session 15 facts remain: [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir]] is dead, [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]] survived, [[People/NPCs/Dalakhar]] is dead, [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]] identified Dalakhar, a red-caped nimblewright caused the blast and searched Dalakhar, [[Objects/Broken Necklace of Fireballs]] remains hidden by [[People/PCs/Broethuul]], and [[People/PCs/Davy]] has Dalakhar's five-gem pouch.
- The Sea Maidens Fair endpoint is no longer table truth. The party did not go to the Dock Ward after the Fireball.
- Aladeen now has table-truth witness involvement in the Fireball aftermath and personally saw Davy pocket something from Dalakhar.
- Urstul's status changed: he was arrested by the Watch, but did not remain in custody. A Manshoon-Zhentarim team intercepted the Watch wagon and escaped with him.

## Starting State

The session began shortly after the immediate rescue work outside [[Places/Trollskull Manor]]. Squiddly was awake and concussed, Fala had been pulled from the rubble dead, the Watch had not yet fully locked down the alley, and the tavern's front room was becoming an improvised triage space.

Aladeen surveyed the scene while the rest of the party continued the lightly retconned actions from Session 15: Broethuul and Elior worked toward the rooftop route, Davy investigated the dead gnome, Jonathan checked the cellar and helped triage, and the Watch was still en route.

## Aladeen's Replay

Aladeen first checked on the urchins. [[People/NPCs/Jenks]] fear-talked through several wild theories, [[People/NPCs/Nat]] signed between the others, and Squiddly remained foggy and fixated on someone wearing red.

Aladeen then comforted a crying boy named Martem, who had been watching the halfling musicians killed in the blast. The boy had parents elsewhere in the North Ward and was sent toward Trollskull's triage area for help.

Aladeen backed up Elior while Elior redirected the badly burned suspicious man toward Trollskull's triage. That man was later recognized as Urstul Floxin.

Aladeen questioned [[People/NPCs/Vincent Trench]] and Vincent's unnamed high-class client. The client blurted out that she saw a crazy puppet on the roof throw something before the explosion. Vincent clarified that it was not a puppet because it had no strings. Together they described a man-sized wood-and-metal figure in a red cloak, bright/white clothing, a large bright hat, and a carved beard. They saw it descend to the blast site, bend over the dead gnome, and then flee east.

At Dalakhar's body, Aladeen saw Davy take a pouch from the dead gnome and also saw Davy realize a civilian had spotted him. Aladeen made a mental note to address it later.

## Watch And Renaer

[[People/NPCs/Saeth Cromley]] and [[People/NPCs/Barnibus Blastwind]] arrived with the Watch investigation. Aladeen listened in while helping around the scene, but his first pass picked up more street rumors than reliable information: Red Wizards, dragons, puppets, Neverwinter politics, and other panic-theories.

Renaer arrived soon after the Watch cordon and identified Dalakhar as an agent of Dagult Neverember. Renaer said Dalakhar had been in Waterdeep for a while, had followed him poorly on Dagult's behalf, and had asked to meet at Trollskull despite Renaer's estrangement from his father. Aladeen immediately floated the idea of using the party's powerful contacts instead of simply chasing random nimblewright leads.

Cromley later took Aladeen's statement. Aladeen told only the clean version: he was inside, came out after the blast, and started helping people. He did not volunteer the puppet/nimblewright details, Davy's theft, or other independently gathered information. He tried to get Cromley to share the Watch's findings, but Cromley refused while reassuring him the Watch would keep the tavern safe.

## Leads Reframed

Once the party compared notes, they still had the core Fireball leads:

- The culprit was almost certainly a nimblewright.
- The nimblewright had a distinctive red cape, big hat, carved beard, and bright clothing.
- The dead gnome was tied to Dagult Neverember and had likely been the gnome referenced in the opened Zhent tube message.
- The dead thugs near him had flying-snake tattoos.
- The House of Inspired Hands had nimblewrights.
- Bojentra Summertaen of the Watchful Order, Lord Deslinter, Davin Crommor, and other early noble adopters were known or rumored nimblewright owners.
- Zardoz Zord remained a possible Luskan source lead, but the party did not pursue him this session.

Aladeen suggested starting with [[People/NPCs/Vajra Safahr]] and then contacting the Harpers, rather than going straight to the docks.

## Frewn Counter-Rumor

As the party prepared to leave, [[People/NPCs/Emmet Frewn]] was loudly telling anyone who would listen that Trollskull brought gang violence to the alley. Aladeen countered just as loudly by implying that the dead flying-snake thugs had been seen drinking at Frewn's place. The performance was good enough that Barnibus moved away from Frewn and noted the claim, leaving Frewn angry and redoubling his accusations.

## Blackstaff Tower

The party went to Blackstaff Tower around noon, buying food on the way through the market. Aladeen also bought a healer's kit for 5 gp to bring back toward the wounded.

Vajra received the group immediately. She already knew a Fireball had gone off in Trollskull Alley. The party reported the nimblewright, Dalakhar's tie to Dagult Neverember, the dead flying-snake thugs, and the possibility of traveling to Neverwinter for answers.

Vajra stiffened at Dagult Neverember's name and sent a paper bird to [[People/NPCs/Laeral Silverhand]], saying the Open Lord needed to be informed if Neverember politics might be involved. She warned that even a cold war between Waterdeep and Neverwinter would be dangerous and that the Closed Council and Open Lord would handle that level of politics.

Aladeen asked whether Vajra could introduce the party to Laeral. With a strong persuasion effort, Vajra agreed to pass along the source of the information, but said any meeting would be Laeral's decision.

Vajra gave the party useful nimblewright context:

- The House of Inspired Hands has several nimblewrights.
- Nimblewrights recently went from rare noble fashion to suddenly more affordable and widespread.
- Early owners included Bojentra Summertaen, Lord Deslinter, and [[People/NPCs/Davin Crommor]].
- The City Armory owns one, but bought later.
- Nimblewrights are bespoke, so the culprit's exact look may identify an owner or source.

## Harper Message

After leaving Blackstaff Tower, the party decided to contact Remallia. Aladeen had a paper bird in his inventory and used it to send a concise report: Fireball at Trollskull Alley, dead and wounded, a rock gnome tied to Dagult Neverember, and a nimblewright attacker. The message was signed from the Skullduggers.

The party did not go to Ulbrinter Villa and did not yet receive an answer before the next crisis began.

## Urstul Extraction

On the way back toward Trollskull, the party saw a Watch prisoner wagon being stopped on a bridge or raised street crossing by three armed thugs. Two Watch guards from the Fireball scene were escorting the wagon. Three attackers blocked the way: two large thugs and a smaller, more polished leader later addressed as Agorn. A fourth, corpse-like spellcaster with a flying-snake tattoo was positioned near the wagon.

The prisoner inside was Urstul Floxin.

The party intervened before the Watch handed over the wagon:

- Aladeen and Davy opened with crossbow shots at Agorn.
- Jonathan climbed up behind the attackers and engaged the spellcaster.
- Elior, piloted because Dane was absent, used Ice Knife against the thugs and later the spellcaster.
- Broethuul parkoured up via a bench, flower cart, and awning, destroying the flower cart in the process.
- Broethuul confirmed the spellcaster had a flying-snake tattoo and saw Urstul gripping the wagon bars.

## Wagon Fight

The spellcaster answered Jonathan's Thunderous Smite with thunder magic of her own, knocking him back, then later used Color Spray to blind both Watch guards. Agorn charmed or magically persuaded one guard to hesitate, then cut down the other guard. The wagon bolted forward, trampling the blinded guard and killing him as well.

Both Watch guards died during the extraction.

The party nearly stopped the escape:

- Davy badly injured Agorn with a hidden crossbow shot.
- Broethuul killed one thug and used Blood Curse of the Fallen Puppet to make the dead man strike another attacker before collapsing.
- Elior's Ice Knife burst badly hurt Agorn and the spellcaster as they fled.
- Aladeen cast Sleep into the wagon seat area, incapacitating the spellcaster.
- Jonathan tried to Compel Duel Agorn, failed, then hit him with Toll the Dead.
- Davy repositioned for a long shot and killed the incapacitated spellcaster.

Despite that, Agorn drove the wagon off at speed with Urstul inside. The session ended as the wagon escaped, with Urstul freed from immediate Watch custody and the party left amid dead Watch guards, at least one dead Zhent thug, a dead Zhent spellcaster, and possibly unresolved wounded attackers on the bridge.

## Ending State

- Current in-world time is early afternoon Ches 22, 1492 DR.
- The party is not at the Sea Maidens Fair; that Session 15 endpoint was retconned.
- The party is near the raised street/bridge where the Watch wagon was attacked, on the way back from Blackstaff Tower toward Trollskull.
- Urstul Floxin has escaped Watch custody in a stolen prisoner wagon driven by Agorn.
- Two City Watch guards are dead.
- The party has a table-revealed name, Agorn, for the escape driver/leader.
- A Zhent spellcaster with a flying-snake tattoo is dead.
- At least one Zhent thug is dead; whether any wounded thug remains immediately present should be clarified at the start of next session.
- Vajra has warned Laeral Silverhand about possible Neverember involvement.
- Remallia has been sent a paper bird report from the Skullduggers, but has not yet answered on screen.
- The Zord / Sea Maidens Fair lead remains available but unpursued.
