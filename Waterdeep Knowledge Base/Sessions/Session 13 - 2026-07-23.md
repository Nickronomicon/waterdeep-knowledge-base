# Session 13 - 2026-07-23

Session 13 covers the final run-up to the [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] grand opening and the opening itself on Ches 19, 1492 DR. The session begins with the tavern nearly ready after the renovation time skip and ends upstairs at Trollskull after the party has opened the business, recovered an unopened sealed [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]], and gathered both a Zhentarim-adjacent runner and [[People/NPCs/Thugby]] for immediate follow-up.

## Starting State

- The session began on the morning of Ches 16, 1492 DR, with Trollskull three days from opening.
- [[Places/Frewn's Brews]] had posted rival anti-ghost advertising across the alley.
- The [[Objects/Cassalanter Loan]] was signed, renovation work had advanced, and [[People/NPCs/Corvin Underbough]] and [[People/NPCs/Miri Underbough]] were already hired for the kitchen.
- The party still needed to choose a final public sign, hire front-room staff, and decide how visibly [[People/NPCs/Lif]] would work during service.

## Hiring Decisions

The party used the applicant packet represented by [[Objects/Trollskull Hiring Applications]] and hired four additional employees:

- [[People/NPCs/Lillian Haekin]] for morning bartending, tea service, and polished front-room work.
- [[People/NPCs/Mosp]], a kobold bartender, for bar service and novelty drinks.
- [[People/NPCs/Ryba]] as a server and bar runner.
- [[People/NPCs/Patric Nivka]] as a barhand, server, and general helper.

[[People/PCs/Aladeen]] negotiated the wages sharply. The final front-room staff cost was 4 gp 5 sp per tenday, reduced to 4 gp per tenday if Patric could stay at Trollskull. The party agreed Patric could sleep in the upstairs den or common area for now, while keeping him away from the basement and the [[Places/Trollskull Smuggler's Passage]].

## Name, Sign, And Roles

- The tavern opened under the name Trollskull Manor.
- The party name or house identity on the sign is the Skullduggers.
- [[People/NPCs/Tally Fellbranch]] delivered and hung the finished sign on the morning of Ches 19: a shield-like sign with a troll skull, crossed swords, "Trollskull Manor" above, and "Skullduggers" below.
- Lif changed his chalkboard "Welcome" to "Open?" before opening. [[People/PCs/Davy]] erased the question mark and underlined the word.
- Mugs turned themselves handle-out behind the bar when the tavern opened.

Opening roles:

- Aladeen handled music, advertisement, and entertainment.
- [[People/PCs/Elior Thalosmere]] acted as social host and maitre d'.
- [[People/PCs/Broethuul]] worked the floor with feathers, social energy, and service support.
- [[People/PCs/Jonathan Usman Sonny Theodore Icarus Charles Eagleshield]] worked the door.
- Davy worked as an unobtrusive busboy and cater-waiter.
- [[People/NPCs/Nat]], [[People/NPCs/Jenks]], and [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]] advertised outside with a ghost-positive jingle and later kept watch in their own conspiratorial way.
- Lif served as a dramatic invisible bartender and proof of concept for the haunted tavern.

## Grand Opening

The opening drew neighbors, guild workers, common customers, and several notable visitors:

- [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir]] visited repeatedly beforehand, helped dress the tavern with festive herbs, and continued chalkboard communication with Lif.
- [[People/NPCs/Tally Fellbranch]] and apprentices installed the sign.
- [[People/NPCs/Broxley Fairkettle]] inspected for the [[Factions/Fellowship of Innkeepers]]. The major practical issue was that operating hours were not yet posted.
- [[People/NPCs/Hammond Craddock]] of the Vintners', Distillers', and Brewers' Guild sampled stock and tried to upsell fresher and more exotic supply.
- [[People/NPCs/Embric]] and [[People/NPCs/Avi]] drank and danced.
- Tally, Fala, and [[People/NPCs/Vincent Trench]] sat together; Vincent watched the room and alley with quiet attention.
- [[People/NPCs/Rishaal the Page-Turner]] read in the taproom and left a twine-wrapped booklet marked "For Lif" on the bar.
- [[People/NPCs/Threestrings]] appeared later and played by the fireplace.
- [[People/NPCs/Laraelra Harsard]] attended in a personal capacity, reinforcing the party's standing with [[Factions/Gray Hands]] without making the opening an official Blackstaff event.
- [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]], [[People/NPCs/Floon Blagmaar]], and [[People/NPCs/Volo]] arrived together. Volo proposed using Trollskull as a stop on a future signing circuit for a book about spirits.
- A silver raven delivered an unsigned congratulatory note likely from [[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree]], keeping Harper support deniable.

## Frewn's Petty Sabotage

[[People/NPCs/Emmet Frewn]] watched the opening from across the alley. Jonathan confronted him, but Frewn avoided doing anything provable in front of witnesses.

A planted customer then loudly complained outside about undead service, poured out his drink in front of the tavern, and tried to reinforce Frewn's anti-ghost rumors. This kept the Frewn rivalry active but still plausibly deniable and petty.

## Staff Performance

- Lillian was positioned for calm, polished service, especially regular morning and tea service.
- Ryba showed strong front-room instincts and took orders effectively from memory.
- Patric worked hard but was inefficient and inexperienced.
- Mosp handled normal drinks adequately but had alarming instincts when customers asked him to "surprise me."
- Lif was highly effective behind the bar, moving drinks quickly and theatrically, though customers still need an obvious way to address a bartender who is usually not visible.
- Corvin and Miri held the kitchen together through opening service.

## Street-War Interruption

During the evening phase, a bloodied Zhentarim-adjacent runner entered Trollskull and asked Jonathan to hide him. Outside, pursuers threatened to burn the place down if he did not come out.

Aladeen cast Suggestion on the runner, compelling him to go outside and stand directly behind Jonathan for eight hours. Outside the tavern, the party faced three armed attackers and a hovering gazer scout tied to [[Factions/Xanathar Guild]] pressure. The attackers claimed the runner was a spy, thief, and liar, and that this was guild business.

Combat followed:

- Elior opened with Ice Knife and later used Thunderwave, killing or badly disrupting enemies while also taking a serious hit.
- Davy shot at the gazer, later landing a hard shot as it retreated over a roof; whether it died or escaped is uncertain.
- Broethuul engaged the bruiser, was badly hit, and later killed the prone lead attacker with his rapier.
- Jonathan held the front line, used Wrathful Smite, and killed the final fleeing cutter with a divine smite.
- Aladeen used Vicious Mockery, Bardic Inspiration, crossbow fire, and Healing Word.
- A cutter snatched the sealed tube from the runner and tried to run with it, but Jonathan killed him and the party recovered the tube.

The crowd watched the fight from the tavern and alley, then cheered once the party won. The Watch was summoned, and multiple bystanders likely reported the violence before the party's own report reached them.

## The Sealed Tube

The recovered [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]] is sealed with a black flying snake with wings. The runner warned the party not to open it, saying that if the message appeared unsealed everyone would have a problem.

The tube was not opened by the end of the session. Its contents are still unrevealed at the table.

## Late-Session Guests And Warnings

- Renaer privately congratulated the party and warned that Neverember agents may be looking for him and could show up around the tavern.
- Floon was drunk, dramatic, and delighted to retell the rescue story, especially around Jonathan.
- Volo continued treating Trollskull as a perfect venue for future spirit-related book publicity.
- Elra made it clear that the party's Force Grey work has been noticed favorably and that discretion matters.
- Thugby accidentally visited the new tavern without realizing it belonged to the party. Aladeen recognized him and persuaded him upstairs for a special drink, leaving him available for questioning.

## Ending State

- The current in-world date is the evening or night of Ches 19, 1492 DR.
- Trollskull Manor is open.
- The party identity on the sign is the Skullduggers.
- The first business results were rolled at the table. After all repairs and two good opening-day performance/business rolls, the Skullduggers had 637 gp left from their original 2,000 gp Cassalanter loan; exact roll totals are not recorded in the vault.
- The Watch response to the bodies outside Trollskull is in progress or immediately pending.
- The Zhentarim-adjacent runner remains magically compelled to stay close to Jonathan.
- Thugby is upstairs as a guest who knows enough to be useful and enough to be nervous.
- The sealed message tube is recovered but unopened.
- The Fireball event is still future-facing and scheduled for Ches 22, 1492 DR per campaign planning.

## Follow-Up

- Resolve the runner, Thugby, and the sealed tube at the start of the next session.
- Decide whether the party opens, delivers, copies, hides, or bargains with the tube.
- Confirm Trollskull's posted operating hours.
- Decide how the Watch records the street-war fight outside an open business.
- Update staff routines, lodging, wages, and customer-facing Lif instructions.
- Keep Frewn's rivalry petty and long-tail unless player actions escalate it.
