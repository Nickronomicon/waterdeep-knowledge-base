# Session 15 - 2026-08-20

Session 15 opens on the Ches 22 Fireball outside [[Places/Trollskull Manor]], resolves the first rescue and triage beats, establishes that [[People/NPCs/Dalakhar]] was killed while trying to meet [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]], identifies a red-clad nimblewright as the attacker, and ends with the party at [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair]] trying to reach [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]].

## Retcon Note

This was an abbreviated, low-energy session right before a several-week real-time gap, and [[People/PCs/Aladeen]]'s player was absent. Aladeen was lightly piloted for healing and witness-gathering. The table may rewind or replay some Session 15 choices in Session 16, especially the shift from Trollskull investigation to the Sea Maidens Fair and any Aladeen-facing social choices.

Until Session 16 confirms the endpoint, treat the broad facts below as table truth but keep evidence custody, witness statements, and the Sea Maidens Fair approach retcon-vulnerable.

## Starting State

- The session began mid-morning on Ches 22, 1492 DR, seconds after the Fireball cliffhanger from [[Sessions/Session 14 - 2026-08-06]].
- The party had completed a long rest after rescuing [[People/NPCs/Nat]] from [[Places/Yellowspire]].
- Trollskull's new windows were shattered inward by the blast.
- The Fireball centered in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]], not inside the tavern.

## Immediate Rescue

The party rushed outside into chaos. Three urgent scenes stood out:

- [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir]] was trapped under collapsed masonry at [[Places/Corellon's Crown]], with [[People/NPCs/Tally Fellbranch]] and apprentices digging frantically.
- [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]] was trapped beneath burning cart and awning debris while Nat and [[People/NPCs/Jenks]] screamed for help.
- Multiple bystanders were dead or badly hurt near the blast center.

[[People/PCs/Elior Thalosmere]] used Thunderwave to clear rubble from Fala, pulling Tally and the apprentices back first. The spell moved the debris but also tumbled Fala's body with it. Elior later tried healing them and realized there was no life left to receive the magic.

[[People/PCs/Jonathan Usman Sonny Theodore Icarus Charles Eagleshield]] lifted the burning cart from Squiddly. [[People/PCs/Davy]] and the other urchins pulled Squiddly free, and Aladeen used Healing Word to bring Squiddly awake with more healing than the boy probably had hit points. Squiddly survived but remained badly disoriented and concussed.

Davy then smothered the largest fire from spilled lamp oil and burning awning cloth. Jonathan checked the tavern cellar and [[Places/Trollskull Smuggler's Passage]] for signs that someone had used the passage; he found no disturbance.

## Casualties And Triage

The confirmed dead count established at the table was eleven:

- [[People/NPCs/Dalakhar]], a male rock gnome near the blast center.
- Two human thug types near Dalakhar, at least one with a black flying-snake tattoo.
- Two humans and one half-elf in servant clothing.
- Four halfling street musicians.
- [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir]].

At least twenty other people in the alley had injuries serious enough to need attention. Trollskull staff quickly turned the tavern into an informal triage space:

- [[People/NPCs/Ryba]] began bringing the badly injured into the front room.
- The kitchen staff boiled water and tore cloth for bandages.
- [[People/NPCs/Patric Nivka]] ran to find the Watch.
- Jonathan helped with battlefield first aid.

[[People/NPCs/Lif]] could not see outside, but asked by chalkboard what had happened and whether Fala was okay. When Broethuul told him Fala had died, Lif froze, snapped the chalk, threw the chalkboard across the room, rattled the bar's glassware, and then went silent.

## First Investigation

[[People/PCs/Broethuul]] scanned the alley and concluded that Dalakhar and the two thugs were probably the intended targets. He also noticed a dangerous-looking burned man trying to leave, and a higher-class woman dressed down and speaking with [[People/NPCs/Vincent Trench]].

Elior examined the blast with Arcana and identified it as a Fireball spell or Fireball-like magic, probably launched from an elevated position. He later inspected the blast center and concluded:

- Dalakhar's pockets had been searched after the explosion.
- Dalakhar appeared to have been running toward Trollskull or that end of the alley.
- The two thugs seemed to be pursuing him.

The dead gnome's loose belongings included a dagger, scraps of string, a handkerchief, a pouch, and a folded note reading: "Trollskull mid-morning." Davy pocketed the pouch of five gemstones while pretending to organize the evidence. A bystander saw him take it.

## Witnesses

Broethuul spoke with the higher-class woman beside Vincent Trench. She said she saw a man-sized puppet without strings on the rooftops. It was wood and metal, dressed in bright clothing, with a big white hat and a beard carved into its face. She saw it throw something; then the street exploded. Vincent kept her identity private and asked that she had never been there.

Broethuul and Elior climbed to the likely rooftop launch point. They found a probable route across roof tiles, where missing or disturbed tiles formed a line, then dropped toward a rain barrel and a crying boy.

The boy, [[People/NPCs/Martem Trec|Martem]], had been watching his halfling musician friends when the blast went off. He hid behind the rain barrel, heard something fall, then saw the construct jump down, run past the musicians, search the middle of the blast zone, and leave. Martem described it as a sword fighter with a big hat, feather, bright red cape, and clanking movement. He also had the fallen [[Objects/Broken Necklace of Fireballs]], which Broethuul identified with Arcana as broken but still holding two live beads.

Broethuul took the necklace and persuaded or deceived Martem into not telling the Watch about it. He then hid the necklace in his room.

The burned dangerous-looking man had been brought into Trollskull by Broethuul and partly healed by Jonathan. When he tried to leave, Jonathan physically forced him back into a seat, hurting him badly because of his burns. After more healing, the man confirmed seeing the mechanical figure searching the short man's body and leaving east. He insisted he was only shopping for books. Later, [[People/NPCs/Saeth Cromley]] recognized him as [[People/NPCs/Urstul Floxin]] and had him arrested and taken to the tower for questioning by [[People/NPCs/Hustus Staget]].

## Watch And Renaer

City Guard cordoned the alley first. Around twenty minutes after the blast, the formal Watch detail arrived, led by Saeth Cromley and [[People/NPCs/Barnibus Blastwind]]. Barnibus walked the same investigative path Elior had, reconstructing the magical origin and rooftop angle. Cromley questioned witnesses and took over the scene.

Renaer arrived after the Watch. He had come to Trollskull because he was supposed to meet a young rock gnome there. He recognized the body as Dalakhar, an agent of his father, Dagult Neverember. Renaer said Dalakhar used to follow him poorly on Dagult's behalf, then disappeared weeks ago. Dalakhar had recently sent word that he was carrying something valuable for Dagult but could not deliver it and hoped Renaer would help.

Elior talked privately with Renaer and read back the session 14 tube message. Renaer agreed it now sounded like the message was describing Dalakhar. He denied knowing more and reiterated that he wants nothing to do with Dagult's business.

## Nimblewright Leads

After collecting three witness descriptions, the party identified the attacker as a nimblewright: a wood-and-metal construct, often built as a servant or guard, currently fashionable in Waterdeep and known for loyalty to its owner.

Known or suggested leads by session end:

- [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]], from Luskan, tied to the Sea Maidens Fair and possibly to Luskan-built nimblewrights.
- The Temple of Gond / [[Places/House of Inspired Hands]], which had nimblewrights in the Twin Parades of Selune's Chalice.
- [[Factions/Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors]], because [[People/NPCs/Bojentra Summertaen]] owns one.
- [[People/NPCs/Davin Crommor]] of House Crommor, who recently acquired one as a guard.

Jonathan, unexpectedly, made the key Arcana connection that the descriptions pointed to a nimblewright.

## Sea Maidens Fair

The party chose to pursue Zardoz first. They went to the Dock Ward, where the Sea Maidens Fair was running as a carnival-like shipbuilders' celebration near two docked ships, while the flagship [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair|Eyecatcher]] sat at anchor about a mile out in the harbor.

The docked ships were identified at the table as the Hellraiser and Heartbreaker or Hellbreaker, with some name confusion. The carnival featured stalls, performers, trinkets, food, and shipboard spectacle. Davy bought grilled mackerel for 2 sp.

The party asked around for Zardoz. With Aladeen's piloted Persuasion helping, sailors and carnival workers pointed them toward Captain Fergus, the drunken captain of the Hellraiser, who was eating and drinking in a tent near the pier. The session ended before the conversation with Fergus began.

## Ending State

- Current in-world time is around noon on Ches 22, 1492 DR.
- The party has left Trollskull's immediate aftermath and is at the Sea Maidens Fair in the Dock Ward, about to talk to Captain Fergus for access to Zardoz.
- Fala is dead.
- Squiddly survived but was concussed and shaken.
- Lif knows Fala is dead and reacted with violent grief.
- Trollskull's front room served as triage; the Watch remains at the Fireball scene for the day.
- Dalakhar is dead and identified by Renaer as Dagult Neverember's agent.
- The party knows Dalakhar came to meet Renaer at Trollskull with something valuable tied to Dagult, but they do not yet know the Stone was stolen unless they infer it from prior clues.
- The party knows a red-caped, hat-wearing, goateed nimblewright caused the blast, searched Dalakhar's body, and fled east.
- Broethuul has the broken necklace of fireballs with two remaining beads and concealed it from the Watch.
- Davy has Dalakhar's pouch of five gemstones; at least one civilian saw him take it.
- Urstul Floxin is in Watch custody after being recognized by Cromley.
- Aladeen's exact actions and any social commitments may need confirmation because his player was absent.
