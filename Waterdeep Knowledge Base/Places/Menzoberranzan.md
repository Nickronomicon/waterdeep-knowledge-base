# Menzoberranzan

Menzoberranzan is the Underdark drow city named in [[People/NPCs/Vincent Trench|Vincent Trench's]] warning after he abandoned the party's investigation of [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]] in Session 17. The transcript renders the name in several phonetic forms.

## Table Knowledge

- Trench returned the full 50 gp and said he would not get entangled with the city. He refused to explain further and asked the party to deny his involvement.
- Most of the party recalled the city's association with Lolth-sworn drow; Jonathan did not pass the history check.
- The DM confirmed a dark-elf connection and stressed that the warning does not establish Lolth's direct involvement or orders.
- No specific drow house, operative, or faction name was identified. The party does not yet know [[Factions/Bregan D'aerthe|Bregan D'aerthe's]] identity or Zord's true identity.
