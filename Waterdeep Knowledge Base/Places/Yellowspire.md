# Yellowspire

Yellowspire is an old yellowed-brick tower in the Castle Ward used by a Banite Zhentarim cell connected to [[People/NPCs/Amath Sercent]] and the [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]] fallout.

## Current Status

- Raided by the party on Ches 21, 1492 DR, during [[Sessions/Session 14 - 2026-08-06]].
- [[People/NPCs/Nat]] was rescued from the upper floor.
- Several Banite acolytes and an infernal panther were killed.
- [[People/NPCs/Amath Sercent]] escaped through the basement teleportation circle.
- [[People/NPCs/Oren Fallow]] escaped into the city.
- The party left with the Zhentarim-stamped scroll tube and message.

## Known Layout

- A front entrance opens into an inhabited vestibule with black hand symbols painted on the walls.
- The main occupied level held Amath and several acolytes during the exchange.
- Stairs lead down to a locked basement room containing a permanent teleportation circle.
- Stairs lead up to the hostage area where Nat was held by an acolyte with a knife.

## Table-Revealed Clues

- The black hand symbols are Banite: Bane, lord of tyranny, one of the Dead Three alongside Myrkul and Bhaal.
- The tower is tied to hostile Zhentarim activity, but the table has not yet distinguished exactly how this cell relates to Davil/Yagra or other Zhentarim factions.
- The permanent teleportation circle implies frequent travel to a stable destination and likely connects to higher-level Zhentarim/Manshoon infrastructure.
- Amath explicitly intended to report to Manshoon.
