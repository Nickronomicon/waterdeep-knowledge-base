# The Sleeping Wench

The Sleeping Wench is a Dock Ward tavern and lodging house on Snail Street near Presbo Lane. [[People/NPCs/Oren Fallow]] kept a room there during session 14.

## Session 14

- [[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree|Remallia]] located Oren here through Harper channels after the party used [[Objects/Remallia's Paper Bird]].
- The tavern had a rough Dock Ward crowd but better clientele than the party expected.
- Several patrons wore black flying-snake marks or otherwise had Zhentarim ties.
- Aladeen learned from a table of Zhent-marked drinkers that Oren was upstairs.
- Jonathan kicked open Oren's door and found him in a half-packed room with the badly resealed tube on the bed.
- Oren tried to escape through the window before Jonathan caught him and Aladeen compelled him with Suggestion.
