# Tiger's Eye

Tiger's Eye is a discreet private investigation office in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]], run by [[People/NPCs/Vincent Trench]].

## Table-Facing Description

- Unremarkable building with an orange-and-black cat's-eye sign.
- Door was locked when Aladeen visited, with a bell beside it.
- Interior is a mixed living and work space: well-decorated, dimly lit, with a receiving room and office.

## Current Use

- Potential paid investigation resource for the gazer/Xanathar surveillance around Trollskull Manor.
- Vincent was briefly retained for the Session 17 Zord inquiry, then abandoned it and refunded all 50 gp.

## Session 17

- All five PCs visited the upstairs entrance/reception space after returning from the ambush. The building was undamaged; Trench's earlier client was gone.
- Paid 50 gp to investigate Zord and nimblewright sales; planned to find Margo themselves.
- Trench later returned all 50 gp at Trollskull, refused further involvement, and warned of Menzoberranzan. No commission remains active.
