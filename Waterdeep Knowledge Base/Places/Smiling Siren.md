# Smiling Siren

An older, well-maintained neighborhood theater/nightclub on **Siren Lane off Tharlion Street**, near the Castle Ward market. Patrons supplied the lead in Session 17; the party visited Margo's rehearsal on Ches 23 in Session 18.

## Current Status

- [[People/NPCs/Margo Verida]] rehearsed with a pianist while stagehands hoisted a painted sea-serpent/dragon prop. She sent them away for the party's private inquiry and planned a performance that night.
- Two duergar and a gazer interrupted with a demand that the party come meet their employer. Both duergar and the gazer died; Margo hid backstage and survived.
- The suspended prop was never dropped. Do not assume it or the theater was wrecked; incidental bolt impacts and combat mess were described, with no repair bill.
- Margo delivered [[Objects/Margo's Letter of Introduction]] and retired to her dressing room with Broethuul's purple feather.
- The party looted the attackers and told two patrolmen of a commotion while leaving. Full Watch response and cleanup remain unresolved.
- [[Objects/Xanathar Capture Orders]] linked the attack to the party's gnome/Stone inquiries. No named team leader, outpost, or prisoner was obtained.
