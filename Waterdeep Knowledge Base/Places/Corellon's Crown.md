# Corellon's Crown

Corellon's Crown is [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir|Fala Lefaliir's]] herbalist shop in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]].

## Description

- A stately three-story townhouse.
- The third floor is a bright greenhouse visible from the street.
- The shop contains herbs, aromatics, flowers, and potions.

## Session 4

- Aladeen visited looking for something to improve Trollskull Manor's smell.
- Fala gifted sage and a lavender bundle to help with the manor.

## Session 17

- Fala is dead after the Ches 22 Fireball collapsed the shop corner onto them; the remains were removed before the party returned.
- Fresh cut timber now shores the damaged corner, probably work by Tally or apprentices. The rest of the dark shop was largely tidy, with unfinished mortar-and-pestle work on the counter.
- Davy checked for obvious signs of Fala's spirit and found none. This establishes no visible haunting, not certainty that the spirit is gone; deeper investigation would require Dorgar.
- Business succession, body arrangements, and Fala's research remain unresolved.
