# Sea Maidens Fair

Sea Maidens Fair is the name used in the session 7 transcript for a Luskan-associated ship or attraction tied to [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]]. The exact canonical spelling may need confirmation.

## Current Status

- Three ships recently came into Waterdeep's port for the coming festival season.
- Sea Maidens Fair is the carnival/fair operation attached to three ships. Session 15 established the flagship [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair|Eyecatcher]] is anchored about a mile out, while two other ships are docked near the fair.
- The city has been buzzing about what the ships will show off this time.
- Zardoz Zord said the whole city would know once the Fair was ready.
- In the first version of session 15, the party came here as a nimblewright lead because the Fireball culprit looked Luskan-built, but [[Sessions/Session 16 - 2026-09-24]] retconned that visit.
- Current table truth: the party has not yet visited the fair after the Fireball. Captain Fergus and the dockside inquiry remain available future material.

## Retconned Session 15 Visit

This visit was played in the abbreviated Session 15 branch, then removed from table truth by [[Sessions/Session 16 - 2026-09-24]]. Reuse these details only if the party follows the Zord lead later.

- The fair was operating in the Dock Ward around noon on Ches 22.
- Two ships were docked near the fair; their names were rendered with some confusion as Hellraiser and Heartbreaker/Hellbreaker.
- The Eyecatcher was anchored offshore and apparently does not come into dock.
- Sailors suggested Zord may use magic rather than rowboats to move between ships.
- The dockside fair included stalls, jugglers, sword swallowers, fire breathers, trinkets, fair food, and grilled mackerel.
- Elior bought grilled mackerel for 2 sp.
- In the retconned branch, the party ended the session before speaking with Captain Fergus.
