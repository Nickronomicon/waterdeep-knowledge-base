# Sea Maidens Fair

Sea Maidens Fair is the Luskan-associated three-ship carnival run by [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]]. Introduced socially in Session 7, it became a visited investigation/heist site in Session 18.

## Session 18 Update

- The party actually visited on Ches 23; Session 15’s visit stays retconned. [[People/NPCs/Fergus Crabwater]] directs Hellraiser; [[People/NPCs/Klarr Besham]] commands Heartbreaker with a shoulder tarantula. Eyecatcher lies about a mile offshore.
- Aladeen/Elior used [[Objects/Margo's Letter of Introduction]], met Zord in the aftcastle captain’s cabin, were dismissed, and returned ashore. Elior spotted [[Objects/Zord's Suspected Customer Ledger]] on his desk; identity/contents unverified.
- Davy observed intership launches, supply traffic, similar docked-ship layouts/hold positions, and visible Eyecatcher alerts for yacht-sized or larger vessels, without the same visible response to small rowboats. This is no guarantee of unnoticed access.
- Rough counts: 16-20 crew seen aboard Eyecatcher, 30-40 estimated full complement; 20-30 performers ashore. Jonathan saw heavy Watch presence but no overt attack. Broethuul failed to secure work; troupe/sailors knew one another and apparently employed no locals.
- Carnival runs from mid-morning into evening, with stalls and performances; a beer garden opens later. No interior floorplan, security schedule, or ongoing boarding permission was obtained.
- **Ship-name slip:** an early line calls the docked pair Eyecatcher/Hellraiser. The same scene identifies Heartbreaker and consistently places flagship Eyecatcher offshore. Use Hellraiser/Heartbreaker as the docked pair while retaining this ambiguity.

## Current Status

- Three ships recently came into Waterdeep's port for the coming festival season.
- Sea Maidens Fair is the carnival/fair operation attached to three ships. The offshore Eyecatcher and docked Hellraiser/Heartbreaker were newly established in Session 18; see the current update below.
- The city has been buzzing about what the ships will show off this time.
- Zardoz Zord said the whole city would know once the Fair was ready.
- In the first version of session 15, the party came here as a nimblewright lead because the Fireball culprit looked Luskan-built, but [[Sessions/Session 16 - 2026-09-24]] retconned that visit.
- Current table truth: Session 18 established the first visit after the Fireball, meetings with both captains, and a Zord audience.

## Retconned Session 15 Visit

This visit was played in the abbreviated Session 15 branch, then removed from table truth by [[Sessions/Session 16 - 2026-09-24]]. Reuse these details only if the party follows the Zord lead later.

- The fair was operating in the Dock Ward around noon on Ches 22.
- Two ships were docked near the fair; their names were rendered with some confusion as Hellraiser and Heartbreaker/Hellbreaker.
- The Eyecatcher was anchored offshore and apparently does not come into dock.
- Sailors suggested Zord may use magic rather than rowboats to move between ships.
- The dockside fair included stalls, jugglers, sword swallowers, fire breathers, trinkets, fair food, and grilled mackerel.
- Elior bought grilled mackerel for 2 sp.
- In the retconned branch, the party ended the session before speaking with Captain Fergus.

## Session 17

- No visit occurred. The DM reiterated Zord's three-ship merchant flotilla, fair/parade role, and nimblewright displays.
- Remallia identified Zord as seller of the eight known Luskan-design nimblewrights. Buyer names became the party's proposed score after Trench abandoned the inquiry.
- Zord meeting access, actual records, storage location, and ship security are still unknown to the PCs. Do not restore retconned dockside details as new discoveries.
