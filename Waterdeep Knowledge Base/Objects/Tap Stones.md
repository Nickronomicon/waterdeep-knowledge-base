# Tap Stones

One pair of common homebrew signaling stones looted from a duergar at the Smiling Siren; Elior recorded the pair in his inventory.

## Table Mechanics

- No attunement. Thumb-sized round discs bearing matching carved eye marks.
- Tapping one repeats the pattern on its partner within **60 feet**.
- The receiving noise resembles knuckles on wood and is audible within **10 feet**.
- Covering a stone muffles its sound. No unlimited range, speech transmission, or additional power was established.
- No use or transfer after looting was shown. Associated [[Objects/Xanathar Capture Orders]] came from the same duergar.
