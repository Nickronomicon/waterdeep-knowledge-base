# Margo's Letter of Introduction

Margo's hastily written introduction secured Aladeen and Elior an audience with Zord aboard Eyecatcher on Ches 23.

## Current Status

- Written after the Smiling Siren attack; Broethuul accepted it and gave Margo his purple feather.
- Taken by Aladeen/Elior to [[People/NPCs/Klarr Besham]], who read it and returned it before fetching permission to board.
- Exact text is not available. Do not invent access privileges beyond the audience it secured.
- Zord later refused the inquiry and asked Klarr to tell Margo he wanted a word. The letter is not permission to return or wander aboard.
- Last seen with the visiting pair; final individual holder unspecified.
