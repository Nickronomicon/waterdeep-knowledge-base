# Cassalanter Rescue Offer

Victoro and Ammalia asked the Skullduggers to recover Neverember's gold to save their twins in Session 17. The account is a family claim, not independent proof of the curse's origin or cure.

## Terms And Current Status

- Recover the [[Objects/Stone of Golorr]] and Neverember's hidden **500,000 dragons**.
- Offered reward: forgiveness of the [[Objects/Cassalanter Loan]] plus **10% of the recovered hoard**; 50,000 gp if recovered in full.
- Victoro explicitly said refusal would leave the existing business arrangement intact.
- Elior, Jonathan, and Broethuul promised an answer after consulting the others, no later than the following morning. All five then discussed the offer at Trollskull.
- Internal consensus favored agreeing and pursuing the gold. Davy supports the innocent children but wants to secure the money before choosing its final disposition. **No acceptance was delivered on screen; the loan remains in force.**

## Family's Account

An Asmodean cultist allegedly cursed [[People/NPCs/Terenzio Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Elzerina Cassalanter]] at birth, then killed himself. Their souls are due on their ninth birthday. Household agents reportedly found an Asmodean text proposing **999,999 gold coins**, melted/burned in a ritual pyre in place of the souls. Victoro conceded it might not work. The household allegedly cannot meet the sum even by liquidating its holdings; the hoard would bridge the gap.

Their agents had followed [[People/NPCs/Dalakhar]] believing he held the Stone, a palm-sized rounded knowledge-storing artifact described with three eyes. [[People/NPCs/Willifort Crowelle]] oversees the household network and offered to connect the visitors to agents; no agent interview occurred.

## Deadline And Evidence Gaps

- Table-facing urgency: roughly **twenty days from Ches 22**, birthday described as a week after Founder's Feast, itself about a tenday away.
- Prep names birthday Tarsahk 11 and ritual cutoff Tarsahk 9. These exact dates and the earlier cutoff were not revealed in Session 17. Confirm the calendar rather than silently replacing dialogue with prep.
- Victoro's first confirming cleric was of Ilmater; Willifort's first confirming cleric was of the Dawnfather. This inconsistency remains unresolved.
- No clerical testimony, curse diagnosis, ritual text, or independent provenance was inspected by the PCs.
- Children are reportedly unaware of their curse. Nana was seen caring for them, but no further identity was confirmed.
- No Cassalanter Eye, hidden cult structure, or broader infernal price was revealed. Cross-reference [[Threads/Cassalanter Warning in the Bugle]] as DM-only background.
