# Xanathar Capture Orders

A short note recovered from the duergar carrying [[Objects/Tap Stones]] at the Smiling Siren on Ches 23.

## Revealed Information

- Identifies the Trollskull group as following the gnome.
- Orders one member captured alive and questioned about what they know and where the [[Objects/Stone of Golorr|Stone]] is.
- The party inferred Xanathar was still looking for the Stone and probably was not the Fireball nimblewright's owner. The note is not direct proof of current Stone custody.
- Both duergar and their gazer died. No prisoner, named commander, delivery location, or password was obtained.
- Mentioned as dropped into the table's handouts; no separate raw handout file is present. Exact carrying PC is uncertain.
