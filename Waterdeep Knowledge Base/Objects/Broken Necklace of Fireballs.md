# Broken Necklace of Fireballs

The broken necklace of fireballs is the physical evidence left near the rooftop escape route after the Ches 22 Fireball in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]].

## Current Status

- Found by [[People/NPCs/Martem Trec]] after the nimblewright jumped down from the rooftop and ran through the blast scene.
- [[People/PCs/Broethuul]] identified it with Arcana as a necklace of fireballs with a broken clasp.
- It no longer works as a necklace, but two beads remain active and dangerous.
- Broethuul took it from Martem and told him not to tell the Watch about it.
- Broethuul hid it in his room at [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] before speaking with the Watch.

## Significance

- Strongly supports that the Fireball was caused by an item thrown from the roof, not necessarily by a caster standing in the alley.
- Connects Martem's witness account to Elior's Arcana reconstruction and the rooftop trail.
- If discovered later, it may create legal trouble because it is both evidence and live magical ammunition.

## Open Questions

- Does Broethuul tell the party he has it?
- Does the Watch realize the necklace is missing from the evidence scene?
- Will the party use the remaining beads, turn them over, or keep them hidden?
