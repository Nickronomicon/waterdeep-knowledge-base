# Remallia's Paper Bird

Remallia's Paper Bird is a one-use magical message bird given to the party by [[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree]] in [[Sessions/Session 09 - 2026-05-07]].

## Current Status

- Given to [[People/PCs/Aladeen]] for the party in session 9.
- Used in session 14 to contact Remallia after [[People/NPCs/Nat]] was kidnapped; Remallia replied by silver raven with [[People/NPCs/Oren Fallow|Oren's]] location.
- Session 16 also established that Aladeen had a paper bird in his inventory and used it to report the Fireball/Neverember/nimblewright situation to Remallia, signed by the Skullduggers.
- This creates an inventory continuity question: either the session 16 bird was a second/duplicate bird, or the session 14 use was not reflected in Aladeen's Foundry inventory.

## Open Questions

- Confirm whether the session 16 paper bird was a second bird or a Foundry inventory continuity duplicate.
- Resolved in Session 17: silver-raven intelligence about eight Luskan nimblewrights, all sold by Zord, plus a replacement paper bird.

## Session 17 Replacement

- Remallia's silver raven dropped a fresh blank sheet for Aladeen, explicitly described as replenishing the party's paper bird.
- This replacement remains available at session end; no new message was sent on screen.
- The Session 14/16 inventory ambiguity remains a historical question, but current stock now includes one explicitly delivered replacement.
