# Willifort Crowelle

Willifort Crowelle is the Cassalanter majordomo or senior house servant who delivered the [[Objects/Cassalanter Loan]] documents and gold to [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] in [[Sessions/Session 12 - 2026-07-09]].

## Current Status

- Appeared at Trollskull Manor at midday on Ches 6, 1492 DR, with two porters and the 2,000 gp loan chest.
- Presented the paperwork on behalf of [[People/NPCs/Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Ammalia Cassalanter]].
- Had a polished, snobbish manner, especially around Trollskull's still-rough condition.
- Left after the party signed and the chest was opened.

## Notes

- The transcript renders his name phonetically at points; use Willifort Crowelle from the DM prep unless corrected.
- [[People/NPCs/Tissina Khyret]] was prepared for the scene but did not appear in transcript truth.

## Session 17 Summons And Questions

- Delivered a summons to Trollskull; the noble PCs understood “earliest convenience” as immediate attendance. Broethuul, Elior, and Jonathan complied.
- At the villa, said he witnessed the cultist's ritual and that the twins remain human, with a mostly dormant curse and occasional brimstone smell.
- Claimed a Dawnfather cleric first confirmed the curse. Victoro instead named an Ilmater cleric first; preserve the discrepancy.
- Suggested jealous rivals might resent the household's rise: socially climbing for about twenty years, with conspicuous public success about three years ago. This was Willifort's theory, not established motive.
- Oversees the household agent network. Expressed trust in the old ritual text's provenance, while conceding he could not establish its truth. No agent interview or document inspection occurred.
- Transcript often says “Crowley”; retain the established canonical name. See [[Objects/Cassalanter Rescue Offer]].
