# Terenzio Cassalanter

Terenzio is the young son of [[People/NPCs/Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Ammalia Cassalanter]], twin to [[People/NPCs/Elzerina Cassalanter]]. Introduced at [[Places/Cassalanter Villa]] on Ches 22 in Session 17.

## Session 18 Update

- Session 18’s recap fixes the ninth birthday at **Tarsahk 11**, with the parents’ planned ritual on **Tarsahk 9**. The party sent acceptance on Ches 23. No new scene with the twins or independent curse evidence appeared.

## Current Status

- Almost nine years old according to the parents; the twins look alike and strongly resemble Ammalia.
- Played mock combat and joined a feather-tickling tussle with [[People/PCs/Broethuul]]. They resumed the feather game in the garden after being ushered away by Nana, an elderly tiefling nanny.
- The parents say the twins' souls will be taken by Asmodeus on their ninth birthday because of a cultist's curse. This is their account, not independently verified by the party.
- The children have reportedly not been told about the curse.
- See [[Objects/Cassalanter Rescue Offer]] for the proposed rescue, reward, sent acceptance, named ritual/birthday dates, and unresolved evidence.
