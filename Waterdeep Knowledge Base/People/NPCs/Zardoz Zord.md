# Zardoz Zord

Zardoz Zord is a flamboyant Luskan ship captain or entertainer associated with [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair]]. He appeared at [[Places/Lightsinger Theater]] with [[People/NPCs/Margo Verida]] in [[Sessions/Session 07 - 2026-04-16]].

## Session 18 Update

- Met Aladeen/Elior aboard Eyecatcher via Margo’s letter and Klarr’s escort; remembered their Renaer connection. The visitors described the Fireball and culprit while deliberately withholding the gnome/Stone details.
- Refused access to customer records on grounds of merchant discretion. Elior alleged Watch corruption and threatened reputational harm (Intimidation 23); Zord took the threat seriously, remained unmoved, and dismissed them with an escort ashore.
- Asked Klarr to tell Margo he wanted a word. No retaliation, buyer name, or further bargain occurred.
- Elior saw him glance toward a bound book on the desk when discussing customers/nimblewrights: [[Objects/Zord's Suspected Customer Ledger]], about 70% confidence, unread.
- Public captain persona only. Bregan D’aerthe/Jarlaxle, magical surveillance, and the Gralhund connection remain unrevealed.

## Current Status

- Attended the opera with Margo Verida.
- Introduced himself to Elior and seemed to expect recognition.
- Said he was originally from Luskan and sails up and down the Sword Coast.
- Said three ships are enough for him and that he is in Waterdeep through the festival season.
- Invited attention toward the Sea Maidens Fair, promising the whole city would know when it opened.
- Became an active Fireball investigation lead because the attacking nimblewright appeared to be of Luskan design.
- The party initially went to the Sea Maidens Fair in the abbreviated session 15, but [[Sessions/Session 16 - 2026-09-24]] retconned that endpoint. Session 17 pursued him indirectly through Trench; Session 18 established a direct audience aboard Eyecatcher.

## Table-Facing Impression

The transcript describes him as if a peacock had been polymorphed into a human: extremely flamboyant, richly dressed, with something like a naval or fancy ship-captain style.

## Fireball Lead

- Jonathan recognized the Fireball attacker descriptions as pointing to a nimblewright.
- The party connected nimblewrights to Luskan construction and remembered Zardoz as a Luskan captain associated with the Sea Maidens Fair.
- Retconned branch only: Sea Maidens workers said Zord commands the whole fair and that he does not appear to bring the Eyecatcher into dock.
- Retconned branch only: the party was pointed toward Captain Fergus of the Hellraiser as a route to Zord.

## Session 17 Buyer-Name Investigation

- Remallia's silver raven identified Zord as seller of **all eight known Luskan-design nimblewrights** in Waterdeep; she did not know the buyers.
- The party considered a prospective-buyer cover: Elior buying a surprise parental gift and requesting customer references. They also sought Margo Verida as an indirect contact. Neither approach was executed.
- Vincent Trench was hired to investigate the sales, then returned the full fee and refused to cross [[Places/Menzoberranzan]]. This provides a drow connection, not proof of Zord's identity, a specific drow faction, or Lolth's involvement.
- Obtaining buyer names is the proposed first heist score. At the Session 17 endpoint, no physical customer list, storage location, ship layout, or access route was confirmed.
- No Zord meeting or post-Fireball fair visit occurred in Session 17; both followed in Session 18.
