# Mosp

Mosp is a kobold bartender hired for [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] during [[Sessions/Session 13 - 2026-07-23]].

## Current Status

- Hired for bar work, novelty service, and late-hour energy.
- Worked behind the bar with [[People/NPCs/Lif]] during the grand opening.
- Handles ordinary drink orders well enough.
- Has alarming instincts when asked for surprise drinks, because his sense of what tastes good does not always match mammalian customers.
- Lif tried to head off at least one of Mosp's more questionable recommendations during opening service.

## Open Questions

- What is the safest way to let Mosp develop a house drink with "proper bite"?
- Can Lif, Lillian, or Broethuul help translate Mosp's enthusiasm into drinks customers survive and enjoy?
