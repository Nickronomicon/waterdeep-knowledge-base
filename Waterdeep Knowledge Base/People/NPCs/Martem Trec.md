# Martem Trec

Martem Trec is a young boy who witnessed part of the Ches 22 Fireball aftermath from behind a rain barrel. Transcript renderings vary between Martem, Martim, and Martin; the prep name is Martem Trec.

## Session 15

- Was watching four halfling street musicians, whom he called friends, when the Fireball killed them.
- Hid behind a rain barrel during the blast.
- Heard something fall nearby and found the [[Objects/Broken Necklace of Fireballs]].
- Saw the red-caped construct jump down, run past the dead musicians, go to the blast center, search or do something there, and leave.
- Described the construct as a sword fighter with a big hat, feather, red cape, clanking movement, and a limp after jumping down.
- Gave the necklace to [[People/PCs/Broethuul]], who told him not to tell the Watch about it.
- Was later questioned by [[People/NPCs/Saeth Cromley]] but omitted the necklace and winked at Broethuul.
- Cromley sent a Watch member to escort him home to his parents in the Trade Ward.
- Broethuul gave him a handwritten 10 percent off dinner coupon for Trollskull.
