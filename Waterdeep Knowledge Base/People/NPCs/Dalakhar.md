# Dalakhar

Dalakhar was a male rock gnome agent of Dagult Neverember. He was killed in the Ches 22 Fireball outside [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] while coming to meet [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]].

## Current Status

- Dead in [[Sessions/Session 15 - 2026-08-20]].
- Body was found near the blast center, apparently pursued by two Zhentarim-marked thugs.
- His pockets were searched after the blast by the red-clad nimblewright.
- [[People/PCs/Davy]] took a pouch of five gemstones from his loose belongings.
- A folded note among his effects read: "Trollskull mid-morning."

## Session 15 Reveal

Renaer recognized Dalakhar and said:

- Dalakhar worked for Dagult Neverember.
- Dalakhar used to follow Renaer on Dagult's behalf, not very stealthily.
- Dalakhar disappeared a few weeks earlier.
- Dalakhar had sent word asking to meet at Trollskull because he carried something valuable for Dagult but could not deliver it.

## DM Notes

- Remix truth: Dalakhar had the Stone of Golorr before the Gralhund nimblewright took it.
- Table truth as of session 15: the party knows Dalakhar carried something valuable and was searched, but the Stone itself has not been directly revealed as the stolen item.
