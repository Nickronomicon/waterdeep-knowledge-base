# Saeth Cromley

Saeth Cromley is a City Watch investigator who helped take control of the Ches 22 Fireball scene in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]].

## Session 15

- Arrived with the formal Watch detail after City Guard cordoned the alley.
- Questioned witnesses in [[Places/Trollskull Manor]], including [[People/NPCs/Martem Trec]].
- Sent a Watch member named Dustin to take Martem home and inform his parents.
- Questioned the party about what they knew, whether Trollskull had gang trouble, and whether they had enemies with access to strong magic.
- Recognized [[People/NPCs/Urstul Floxin]] in the tavern triage area and ordered him escorted to the tower for further questioning.

## Session 16

- Took a limited statement from Aladeen during the replayed Fireball investigation; Aladeen admitted he came out after the blast and helped people, but did not volunteer the full nimblewright/Davy details.
- Urstul escaped later that day when Agorn's Zhentarim team hit the Watch prisoner wagon. Cromley may not yet know this at the table, but the Watch will.
