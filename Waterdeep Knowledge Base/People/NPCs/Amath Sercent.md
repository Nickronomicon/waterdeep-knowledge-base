# Amath Sercent

Amath Sercent is a Mulan Banite priestess operating from [[Places/Yellowspire]] and aligned with hostile Zhentarim pressure around [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]].

## Current Status

- Escaped Yellowspire through a permanent basement teleportation circle during [[Sessions/Session 14 - 2026-08-06]].
- Intended to report the party's actions to Manshoon.
- Knows the party broke the tube seal, brought [[People/NPCs/Oren Fallow]] back, fought her acolytes, rescued [[People/NPCs/Nat]], and retained the Zhent-stamped message tube.

## Table-Facing Details

- Bald Mulan woman with no eyebrows or eyelashes, wearing simple but rich robes.
- Addressed by Oren as "Mistress" with a transcript-heard variant close to Sersent/Sursant.
- Commands Banite acolytes and had an infernal panther-like creature available as tower defense.
- Her site uses black hand symbols of Bane, not Asmodean imagery.

## Session 14

- Received the party at Yellowspire after Nat's kidnapping forced the exchange.
- Quieted Oren after he tried to explain that Xanathar's people chased him and the party forced the broken-seal problem.
- Concluded she had Oren, the tube, and the message, then ordered her acolytes to kill the party.
- Was badly injured by Elior's empowered Ice Knife.
- Paralyzed Elior with Hold Person and fled downstairs, telling the room to kill the rest while she reported to Manshoon.
- Escaped via the permanent teleportation circle before the party reached the basement.

## Uncertainties

- Original adventure/prep spelling is Amath Sercent; raw session-14 prep notes used Amath Seccent; transcript speech renders variants. Use Amath Sercent as the canonical page name unless the table explicitly adopts a different spelling.
- Her exact rank within Manshoon's local Zhentarim structure is not yet established by table truth.
- The destination of the permanent teleportation circle has not been revealed.
