# Patric Nivka

Patric Nivka is a young Illuskan barhand and general helper hired for [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] during [[Sessions/Session 13 - 2026-07-23]].

## Current Status

- Hired as a barback, server, porter, and general helper.
- Inexperienced at table service but eager, physically capable, and hard-working.
- The party agreed to let him stay at Trollskull, reducing front-room staffing costs to 4 gp per tenday.
- He is currently allowed to sleep in the upstairs den or common area, not in the basement.
- During opening service, he worked hard but inefficiently and needs training.

## Known Background

- Patric needed work, an advance, a place to sleep, or some combination of the three.
- Table discussion established that he had been kicked out by his parents after a relationship with a half-elven girl.

## Open Questions

- Does Patric become a loyal fixture if treated well?
- How much does staff lodging complicate the party's privacy, hidden passages, and late-night faction business?
