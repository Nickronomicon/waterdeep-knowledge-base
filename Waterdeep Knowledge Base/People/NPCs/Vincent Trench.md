# Vincent Trench

Vincent Trench is the private investigator who runs [[Places/Tiger's Eye]] in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]].

## Current Role

- Discreet information broker/private eye.
- Can potentially investigate the Xanathar-linked gazer watching [[Places/Trollskull Manor]].
- Briefly retained in Session 17 for the Zord/nimblewright inquiry, then withdrew and refunded the fee in full. See the account below.

## Session 6

- Met Aladeen after Aladeen rang the bell at Tiger's Eye.
- Was sharply dressed, pipe-smoking, and looked as if he had just woken up.
- Said that if someone sends a gazer around to look at the party, it always leads back to Xanathar.
- Clarified that he does not know which Xanathar operative is responsible, but could possibly find out for pay.
- Believes the gazer was watching the party, not the alley itself.
- Agreed to warn the party if he sees anything around the alley.

## Session 17 Investigation And Withdrawal

- Hired at [[Places/Tiger's Eye]] to investigate [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]] and Luskan nimblewright sales after receiving the party's Fireball findings. The party chose to locate [[People/NPCs/Margo Verida]] itself.
- Charged 50 gp upfront, 50 gp more if work exceeded a tenday, with a promised half refund if he found nothing. All five PCs paid 10 gp; reports were to go to Trollskull.
- Returned to the upstairs dwelling the same night, refunded **all 50 gp**, and refused to continue. Recommended dropping the Zord investigation.
- Said the party's names had never come up and asked that his own involvement be denied.
- A Persuasion result of 19 elicited only that he would not get entangled with [[Places/Menzoberranzan]]. His discovery, source, and any actual threat remain unknown. No active commission remains.
