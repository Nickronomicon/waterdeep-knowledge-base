# Agorn Fuoco

Agorn Fuoco is a Manshoon-aligned Zhentarim response-team figure. In table truth, the party heard him addressed as "Agorn" during the attack on the Watch wagon carrying [[People/NPCs/Urstul Floxin]] in [[Sessions/Session 16 - 2026-09-24]].

## Current Status

- Alive and escaped at the end of session 16.
- Led or drove the extraction team that intercepted Urstul's Watch wagon.
- Was accompanied by two large thugs and a corpse-like spellcaster with a flying-snake tattoo.
- Took several hits from Aladeen, Davy, Elior, and Jonathan but escaped with the wagon and Urstul.
- Killed one of the two Watch guards after the spellcaster blinded them, then drove the wagon away.

## Table-Facing Facts

- Was the more polished leader/face among the attackers rather than one of the brute thugs.
- Used a short sword and magic; one spell persuaded a Watch guard that Urstul was not worth the fight.
- The spellcaster called him "Agorn" while climbing onto the wagon.
- He told Urstul, "I don't think you're worth this," and Urstul answered that if Agorn got him out, he would make it worth it.

## DM Notes

- [[Reference/Remix Response Teams]] lists Agorn Fuoco as a Manshoon's Zhentarim response-team lead, typically a bard with thugs.
- Preserve the split between this Manshoon/Banite pressure and any Davil/Yagra/Doom Raider Zhentarim relationship until the table proves otherwise.

## Session 17 Aftermath

- Remains escaped with Urstul; no pursuit or new confrontation occurred.
- Opening recap confirmed the mage and both thugs died. The mage's body traveled on the wagon; two thugs and two Watch guards remained at the scene.
- The party looted/dressed the scene and left without notifying the Watch on screen. Agorn's destination and next move remain unknown.
