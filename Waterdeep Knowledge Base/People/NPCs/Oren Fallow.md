# Oren Fallow

Oren Fallow is the Zhentarim-adjacent runner whose sealed message tube triggered the session 14 Yellowspire fallout.

## Current Status

- Escaped from [[Places/Yellowspire]] during the Ches 21 fight.
- Was hit by multiple attacks and crossbow bolts while fleeing, but survived and disappeared into the city.
- No longer has the [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]]; [[People/PCs/Elior Thalosmere]] kept the Zhentarim-stamped tube and message after Yellowspire.

## Session 14

- Entered [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] during the session 13 opening-night street fight while chased by Xanathar-linked attackers.
- At the start of session 14, was still under [[People/PCs/Aladeen|Aladeen's]] Suggestion to stand behind [[People/PCs/Jonathan Usman Sonny Theodore Icarus Charles Eagleshield|Jonathan]].
- Begged the party not to open the sealed tube, saying he did not know the contents but did know the seal and delivery consequences.
- Said most deliveries went to [[Places/Yellowspire]] in the Castle Ward and that this tube had been handed to him in the Trade Ward.
- Panicked and plugged his ears while the party read the message aloud.
- Accepted the badly resealed tube and fled toward the Castle Ward.
- Was later named in [[Objects/Yellowspire Demand Note]] after [[People/NPCs/Nat]] was kidnapped.
- Kept a room at [[Places/The Sleeping Wench]] on Snail Street in the Dock Ward.
- Tried to escape through his room's window when the party found him, but Jonathan caught his ankle and Aladeen compelled him again with Suggestion.
- Was brought back to Trollskull, tied up, and taken to Yellowspire as part of the exchange.
- Tried to explain himself to [[People/NPCs/Amath Sercent]], blaming Xanathar pursuers and the party's compulsion.
- Broke free of his ropes during the Yellowspire fight and ran.

## Uncertainties

- Prep used "Orryn Fallow"; session 14 table text establishes "Oren Fallow" in the delivered note.
- It is not clear whether Oren serves Davil/Yagra's Zhentarim circle, Amath/Manshoon's Banite-aligned cell, or a looser courier layer that crosses cell boundaries.
- His current wounds, hiding place, and next report are unknown.
