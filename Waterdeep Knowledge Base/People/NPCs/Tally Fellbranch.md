# Tally Fellbranch

Talisolvanar "Tally" Fellbranch is the half-elf carpenter and woodcarver who runs [[Places/The Bent Nail]] in [[Places/Trollskull Alley]].

## Current Role

- Neighbor to [[Places/Trollskull Manor]].
- Practical, watchful, and concerned about danger spilling into the alley.
- Brought whiskey to Trollskull Manor after the party returned from the Dock Ward grell fight.
- Knows a long chain of Trollskull Manor history and ghost rumors.
- Making three wooden training bucklers for the urchins, commissioned in session 10.
- Supplied furniture, beams, and apprentices during the session 12 renovation montage.
- Delivered and hung the final Trollskull Manor sign on opening morning in session 13.

## Session 6

- Asked the party whether anything followed them back, because he would rather know before it took an interest in his shutters.
- Shared Trollskull Manor history with [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir]].
- Confirmed he had never met the old Mirthkettle "hag" figure; she was already a story when he arrived.
- Said [[People/NPCs/Lif]] died about forty years ago after a support beam on ale casks collapsed on him.
- Offered to keep an eye out after the party's clue items briefly went missing.
- Play-sword-fought his way through the urchins on his way back to his shop.

## Session 10

- Was working on a hand-carved dining-room table for a Castle Ward client when the party visited.
- Agreed to make three small wooden bucklers for the urchins for 10 gp total, ready the next day.
- Was told after the Cassalanter negotiation that the party would soon have more work for him at Trollskull Manor.

## Session 12

- Brought furniture, new beams, and apprentices into the Trollskull renovation work once the Cassalanter money arrived.
- By Ches 16, still needed the party's final tavern name before completing the signboard.

## Session 13

- Delivered the finished Trollskull Manor sign on Ches 19 with apprentices.
- The sign carries the Skullduggers identity beneath the troll skull and crossed swords.

## Session 17 Whereabouts

- Absent when the party returned after the wagon ambush; Fala's remains had also been removed. The DM inferred he was probably with the remains, without confirming it.
- Fresh timbers shored the damaged corner of Corellon's Crown. Davy suspected Tally or his apprentices did the work; no conversation confirmed who did it.
