# Hammond Craddock

Hammond Craddock is a Vintners', Distillers', and Brewers' Guild representative connected to [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] supply.

## Current Status

- Visited Trollskull during the grand opening in [[Sessions/Session 13 - 2026-07-23]].
- Sampled flights poured by [[People/NPCs/Lif]].
- Tried to upsell newer, fresher, and more exotic stock after pointing out limitations in the opening supply.
- [[People/PCs/Broethuul]] deferred the upsell until the tavern's next order.

## Prep Use

Hammond is a useful mundane pressure valve: supply quality, guild relationships, upsells, shortage rumors, festival pricing, and the gap between what the tavern can afford and what it wants to become.
