# Elzerina Cassalanter

Elzerina is the young daughter of [[People/NPCs/Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Ammalia Cassalanter]], twin to [[People/NPCs/Terenzio Cassalanter]]. Introduced at [[Places/Cassalanter Villa]] on Ches 22 in Session 17.

## Current Status

- Almost nine years old according to the parents; the twins look alike and strongly resemble Ammalia.
- Played mock combat and joined a feather-tickling tussle with [[People/PCs/Broethuul]]. They resumed the feather game in the garden after being ushered away by Nana, an elderly tiefling nanny.
- The parents say the twins' souls will be taken by Asmodeus on their ninth birthday because of a cultist's curse. This is their account, not independently verified by the party.
- The children have reportedly not been told about the curse.
- See [[Objects/Cassalanter Rescue Offer]] for the proposed rescue, reward, pending reply, and unresolved deadline.
