# Davin Crommor

Davin Crommor is a member of House Crommor and a new nimblewright lead from the Ches 22 Fireball investigation.

## Current Status

- Mentioned in session 15 as the owner of a recently acquired nimblewright serving as a guard.
- The party has not yet visited or questioned him.
- This lead is especially sensitive because of [[People/PCs/Davy]]'s private Crommor identity thread.

## DM Notes

- Session 15 prep substituted Davin Crommor for one of the published nimblewright-owner leads so the Fireball investigation can brush Davy's backstory.
- Davin's nimblewright is not the culprit per prep; it is a lead toward Zord and the broader nimblewright network.
