# Margo Verida

Margo Verida is a singer known as the Siren of the Dales. She attended the opera at [[Places/Lightsinger Theater]] during [[Sessions/Session 07 - 2026-04-16]], though she was not performing.

## Current Status

- Attended the opera with [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord]].
- Was recognized by Elior among the notable attendees.
- Elior approached and opened with a poor opera joke; Margo quickly looked to Zardoz to handle the interaction.

## Session 17 Search Lead

- Trollskull patrons described Margo as a famous Waterdeep local whose career took her up and down the Sword Coast. Retired or past her peak, she remains an excellent and recognizable singer.
- Reported sightings cluster in Castle Ward. She occasionally visits [[Places/Smiling Siren]], a nightclub/theater where she has given impromptu performances; she also visits other bars.
- Nobody supplied her residence or a reliable schedule.
- The party wants her insight into Zord before trying to obtain nimblewright buyer names. They chose to find her themselves while hiring Trench to investigate Zord. No meeting occurred.
