# Cassalanters

The Cassalanters are an old Waterdeep noble banking family. They entered table truth directly in [[Sessions/Session 07 - 2026-04-16]], when [[People/NPCs/Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Ammalia Cassalanter]] approached the party at the opera.

## Current Status

- Victoro and Ammalia attended the opera at [[Places/Lightsinger Theater]].
- Victoro recognized or at least noticed Renaer's rescuers and the new owners of [[Places/Trollskull Manor]].
- Victoro offered the party a conversation about a loan for restoring Trollskull Manor.
- The party was invited to [[Places/Cassalanter Villa]] on Diamond Street in the Sea Ward to discuss terms.
- In session 9, [[People/NPCs/Remallia Haventree]] included Victoro on a short lender list or confirmed him as a practical loan contact while asking the party not to use her name.
- In session 10, Aladeen, Broethuul, and Jonathan visited Cassalanter Villa and negotiated the [[Objects/Cassalanter Loan]].
- In session 12, [[People/NPCs/Willifort Crowelle]] delivered the documents and funds to Trollskull, and all five PCs signed the loan.

## Table-Facing Impression

- Victoro was polished, confident, and socially selective, shaking hands with the human men while giving Broethuul an odd look and overlooking Davy.
- Ammalia projected strong noble presence and social gravity.
- An older tiefling woman in black attended Ammalia but was not introduced.

## DM-Facing Relevance

The Cassalanters are already tied to the campaign's private Crommor material through [[Threads/Cassalanter Warning in the Bugle]], while the later Session 17 scene adds their claimed family curse and rescue proposal without revealing the deeper Crommor/infernal connections.

## Open Questions

- Does Victoro recognize any deeper Crommor connections in Jonathan, Aladeen, or Davy?
- Who was the tiefling attendant with Ammalia?
- Why did the prepared Tissina Khyret not appear with Willifort in transcript truth, and should she still enter play later?
- How will the Cassalanters use the party's debt once Trollskull opens?

## Session 17 Rescue Request

- Ammalia and Victoro received Broethuul, Elior, and Jonathan at [[Places/Cassalanter Villa]], where they met the twins [[People/NPCs/Terenzio Cassalanter]] and [[People/NPCs/Elzerina Cassalanter]]. The visitors later briefed the whole party.
- The parents claimed a cultist cursed the twins' souls at birth and that they need **999,999 gold coins** for a ritual before the children's ninth birthday, roughly twenty days away by dialogue. Neither the curse's origin nor the cure was independently verified.
- Named Neverember's Enigma as **500,000 hidden embezzled dragons**, the [[Objects/Stone of Golorr]] as the key, and Dalakhar as their agents' last Stone lead.
- Offered loan forgiveness plus 10% of the hoard for help. Said a refusal would leave the existing loan arrangement intact.
- Visitors promised an answer by the next morning. Internal party consensus later favored pursuing the gold and agreeing to help, with Davy reserving judgment over its final disposition. No reply reached the family on screen.
- [[Objects/Cassalanter Rescue Offer]] holds the full terms, clerical-account discrepancy, and unresolved deadline. No household Eye or hidden cult operation was revealed.
