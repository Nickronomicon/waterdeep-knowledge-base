# Zhentarim

The Zhentarim, also called the Black Network or "the Zhents" at the table, are an organized criminal faction active in Waterdeep's underworld.

## Current Campaign State

- Openly fighting the [[Factions/Xanathar Guild]] in the streets as of [[Sessions/Session 02 - 2026-02-22]].
- Marked by black flying-snake tattoos.
- Abducted [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]] and [[People/NPCs/Floon Blagmaar]] outside [[Places/Old Xoblob Shop]].
- Used [[Places/Candle Lane Zhentarim Warehouse]] as a safe house or holding location.
- Questioned Renaer about Dagult Neverember and the rumored cache of embezzled dragons.
- Were then hit by a Xanathar-linked raid that took Floon.
- [[People/NPCs/Mirt]] says [[People/NPCs/Maxeene]] overheard useful talk from Zhentarim operatives.
- The [[Objects/Scrap of Note]] includes "Z" in a context the party speculated might mean Zhentarim, but that is not confirmed.
- [[People/NPCs/Maxeene]] reported that [[People/NPCs/Davil Starsong]] and [[People/NPCs/Yagra Stonefist]] discussed hiring spies to find [[Factions/Xanathar Guild]] hideouts.
- A poison-weapon attacker at [[Places/Thomril & Sons Cartage]] had a flying-snake tattoo.
- [[People/NPCs/Thugby]] reappeared among the arson crew at Thomril & Sons, then fled after recognizing the party.
- Session 12 did not advance the Zhentarim lead directly, but the Xanathar-linked harbor fight may matter to Davil/Yagra's anti-Xanathar spy effort.
- In session 13, a Zhentarim-adjacent runner with a sealed black flying-snake message tube fled into Trollskull Manor during the grand opening.
- The party recovered the [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]] after a Xanathar-linked cutter tried to steal it.
- In session 14, the runner was named [[People/NPCs/Oren Fallow]]. The party opened the tube, learned it concerned a living gnome, the Stone, missing eyes, old Neverember channels, Xanathar watchers, the lord's son, and the Portal, then released Oren with a bad reseal.
- Hostile Zhentarim retaliated by kidnapping [[People/NPCs/Nat]] and sending [[Objects/Yellowspire Demand Note]].
- [[Places/Yellowspire]] proved to be a Banite Zhentarim outpost led by [[People/NPCs/Amath Sercent]] and tied explicitly to Manshoon by Amath's intended report.
- Nat was rescued, but both Oren and Amath escaped; the party retained the tube/message.

## Open Questions

- Which local Zhentarim cell staged the abduction?
- Who ordered the search for Renaer and the locket contents?
- How does this cell relate to Davil Starsong and other possible Zhentarim factions?
- Did Davil Starsong, Yagra Stonefist, or another Zhentarim cell order the attack on Thomril & Sons?
- Does "Z" in the Scrap of Note mean Zhentarim?
- Are Davil's people the same Zhentarim faction that abducted Renaer and Floon, or a rival/less-hostile splinter?
- Do `D.` and `Y.` in the opened tube message point to [[People/NPCs/Davil Starsong]] and [[People/NPCs/Yagra Stonefist]], or is that a dangerous false association?
- How does Davil/Yagra's cell react if the party asks about Oren, Yellowspire, Amath, Manshoon, or the Banite symbols?
- Where did Oren flee after Yellowspire, and who reaches him first?
- What does Amath report to Manshoon, and what response follows?
