# Session Prep - 2026-08-06

Session 14 starts in the immediate fallout of Trollskull Manor's grand opening and should end on Ches 22, 1492 DR, with the Fireball in Trollskull Alley. The likely arc is: finish the opening-night runner/Watch/tube problem, play one consequence scene on Ches 20 or 21, then cut to the morning blast that launches Chapter 3.

## Target End State

- [[People/NPCs/Hustus Staget]] has taken a clean enough Watch statement about the opening-night fight.
- [[People/NPCs/Thugby]] is resolved as a frightened red herring, not the source of the new Zhentarim-Xanathar clash.
- The party has chosen what to do with the Zhentarim-adjacent runner and [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]].
- If the tube is opened, Zhentarim retaliation points them at Yellowspire in the Castle Ward.
- If the tube remains sealed, the Harpers provide a short monster-facing mission that seeds Asmodean cult activity without pointing directly at [[Places/Cassalanter Villa]].
- The session ends with the Fireball explosion itself, before any explanation, casualty count, or investigation.

## Opening Recap Bullets

- Trollskull Manor opened on Ches 19 under the Skullduggers sign.
- Opening day went well; after repairs and two strong opening rolls, the party has 637 gp left from the original Cassalanter loan reserve.
- A Zhentarim-adjacent runner fled into the tavern, pursued by Xanathar-linked attackers and a gazer.
- The party killed the attackers outside, recovered the sealed black flying-snake tube, and called the Watch.
- The runner is under Aladeen's Suggestion and stuck near Jonathan for eight hours unless harmed by the party.
- Thugby accidentally came to the opening and is upstairs, terrified.
- Renaer, Floon, and Elra are still downstairs as the Watch approaches.

## Current Party State

- Level 3, at [[Places/Trollskull Manor]] on the evening/night of Ches 19.
- Public goodwill is high after the opening and the alley fight, but the Watch must still account for bodies outside a tavern.
- Staff are tired, new, and watching how the owners handle danger.
- Lif can help with silent service, locked doors, chalkboard messages, and theatrical room control, but cannot leave the manor.
- The first regular tenday business roll is not due until about Ches 29; do not roll it tonight unless the calendar jumps much farther than planned.

## Scene 1: Upstairs With The Runner And Thugby

### The Runner

Use **Orryn Fallow** as the runner's name unless table improv lands on something better. He is a young human courier with a split lip, alley grime on his cuffs, and the desperate confidence of someone who knows his bosses are scarier than the people in front of him.

Orryn's priorities:

- Stay close to Jonathan until Suggestion ends.
- Get the tube delivered with its seal intact.
- Avoid admitting who hired him unless clearly protected from both gangs.
- Keep the party from opening the tube by stressing that a broken seal means retaliation.

What Orryn knows:

- He was paid to move the sealed tube from a lower-level Zhentarim contact to a handoff near the Yawning Portal.
- He does not know the full Grand Game, but he heard the words "gnome," "stone," "Neverember channels," and "old road" before being sent out.
- Xanathar people intercepted him because they believed the tube contained the current route to the gnome.
- The seal is a proof-of-custody mark; if it breaks, the intended receiver assumes the courier, carrier, or thieves read it.
- The people behind this job are not the same as every Zhent in Waterdeep. If the party names [[People/NPCs/Davil Starsong]] or [[People/NPCs/Yagra Stonefist]], Orryn becomes visibly unsure.

If magically compelled:

- He can give impressions and fragments, not a perfect conspiracy map.
- He names "Yellowspire" only if pressed about where broken-seal problems are handled.
- He says "Dalakhar" as a name he overheard only on a strong interrogation result or if the party has already opened the tube.

### Thugby

Thugby is a red herring. His primary goal is to leave alive without being dragged into whatever the party has decided is happening.

What he can truthfully say:

- He heard there was a new tavern opening and came for a drink, not a job.
- He has mostly avoided serious work since the Thomril & Sons disaster because "you people keep being at crimes."
- He does not know Orryn, the tube, Yellowspire, Dalakhar, or the current street-war clash.
- He knows the Thomril arson crew was hired through tavern-and-alley middlemen, not a face-to-face boss.
- He is terrified of Jonathan and Aladeen, and he expects Broethuul to stab him if he says the wrong thing.

Useful ruling: if the party spares him cleanly, he can become a low-grade rumor source later. If they threaten him too hard, he disappears from easy contact.

## Scene 2: Staget And The Watch

Staget arrives with a small Watch detail. He remembers the party from Candle Lane and is not surprised they have found the exact middle of another gang problem.

Staget's read:

- Witnesses saw attackers threaten the tavern and saw the party defend the alley.
- Bodies outside an open business still require names, statements, and a Watch report.
- He is willing to treat this as gang violence spilling into Trollskull, provided the party does not lie badly, hide corpses, or assault the runner in front of witnesses.
- He wants the runner alive if possible.
- He is interested in the sealed tube as evidence, but may accept a sworn statement that the party will preserve it if Renaer, Elra, or Jonathan leans on social credibility.

Fast checks:

| PC Action | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Give a clean account | Persuasion DC 13 | Staget records the party as defenders and leaves legal heat at a warning. |
| Conceal the tube | Deception or Sleight of Hand DC 15 | Success keeps it out of Watch custody; failure makes Staget colder and more procedural. |
| Argue legal self-defense | History or Persuasion DC 14 | Staget accepts the Code Legal framing but warns that the next incident at Trollskull will be harder to wave away. |
| Use Renaer or Elra as witnesses | Persuasion DC 12 | Advantage if the party has been honest with them. |
| Hand over runner and tube | No roll if cooperative | Watch takes custody; branch to unopened-tube lane unless the party secretly copied/opened it first. |

Staget snippets:

- "You have a gift for standing exactly where Waterdeep is bleeding."
- "I am not here to punish a tavern for defending itself. I am here because corpses require paperwork."
- "If that tube is evidence, I want it intact. If it is bait, I want it nowhere near children."
- "Lord Eagleshield, noble names make statements louder, not more true."

If the party opens the tube, reveal [[Objects/Zhent Runner Message Tube]].

Opening the tube advances:

- [[Factions/Zhentarim]] split pressure.
- [[Factions/Xanathar Guild]] attention.
- [[Threads/Great Game]] visibility.
- Dalakhar setup for Ches 22.

If the party keeps the tube sealed:

- Let Staget take it, or let Orryn flee with it if they actively release him.
- Keep Dalakhar moving offscreen.
- Use the Harper monster mission below to fill Ches 20-21 and seed Cassalanter/Asmodean pressure.

## Branch A: The Tube Is Opened

### Retaliation Hook: Nat Missing

Recommended choice: take only [[People/NPCs/Nat]]. It keeps the threat personal while leaving [[People/NPCs/Jenks]] and [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]] able to report what happened.

On Ches 20 or early Ches 21:

- Tally arrives with the three wooden bucklers Aladeen commissioned.
- Jenks and Squiddly are late, filthy, and frantic; Nat was grabbed after the three tried to follow a black flying snake "because it looked like one of the bad marks."
- A second flying snake drops [[Objects/Yellowspire Demand Note]] at Trollskull.
- The demand requires the runner, the tube, and every copy of the message at Yellowspire before moonrise on Ches 21.

If all three urchins are taken instead:

- Tally delivers the bucklers and realizes the children never came to pick them up.
- The demand note arrives while the party is deciding where to search.
- Use Lif's chalkboard to write "CHILDREN?" if the table needs a knife-twist.

### Yellowspire

Use the Old Tower map from `../raw/waterdeep_dragon_heist/images/map-05.05-old-tower-player.jpg`.

Yellowspire is a pale-yellow brick tower in the Castle Ward. The Banite cell inside calls it the House of Tyranny. This version uses the original adventure's Amath Sercent/Yellowspire material as a remixable outpost, but does not include Shan Chien.

Table note: raw session 14 notes spell the priestess as Amath Seccent; original adventure text spells her Amath Sercent. Use **Amath Sercent** unless the table has already heard otherwise.

#### Situation

- [[Factions/Zhentarim|Manshoon-aligned Zhentarim]] have borrowed Banite muscle to erase everyone tied to the broken tube.
- Amath Sercent intends to trade Nat for the runner/tube, then kill the party inside the tower.
- Amath should survive if possible. She flees through the basement teleportation circle when bloodied, when two acolytes fall, or when the party reaches O5.
- Nat is bound and hidden upstairs behind a torn tapestry or in a curtained alcove. She is frightened, angry, and unharmed at the start of combat.

#### Tower Beats

| Area | Use |
|---|---|
| O1 Vestibule | Peephole, watcher, simple chance to detect ambush posture. |
| O2 Ground Level | Two acolytes dressed as ordinary caretakers; they stall while signaling upstairs. |
| O3 Upper Level | Main ritual/hostage room, black handprints on the walls, Nat hidden but audible if the party listens. |
| O4 Cellar | Rations, cards, spare shackles, one clue packet. |
| O5 Teleportation Circle | Locked door; Amath's escape route toward Kolat/Manshoon infrastructure. |

#### Encounter

Baseline for five level-3 PCs:

- Amath Sercent using priest statistics.
- 3 acolytes.
- 2 flying snakes.
- 1 fiendish temple cat using panther statistics, with fiend creature type and Infernal-flavored eyes.

Adjustments:

- Easier: remove the temple cat and one acolyte.
- Harder: add a fourth acolyte or let the temple cat use pack tactics while an acolyte is adjacent to its target.
- Faster: Amath opens with command or hold person, then flees as soon as the fight turns.

Enemy goals:

- Get the message, all copies, and Orryn.
- Kill Orryn if he is present and the party refuses terms.
- Use Nat as leverage, not as a combat target unless the party's choices make the scene much darker than intended.
- Preserve Amath as a future Manshoon/Banite thread.

#### Yellowspire Clues

Place at least three:

- A scrap naming "Dalakhar" as a rock gnome moving through sewer routes and "Neverember's son's new house" as a likely contact point.
- A courier tally with a black flying-snake seal, matching the opened tube.
- A partial list of watchers: "Renaer, Trollskull, north-side alleys, Portal too exposed."
- A burned note from "U.F." demanding speed because Xanathar's people are close.
- Infernal-looking black handprints are Banite, not Asmodean; Religion DC 13 distinguishes Bane from devil worship and avoids muddying the Cassalanter clue lane.
- The teleportation circle sigils point toward deeper Zhentarim infrastructure, but not enough to raid Kolat Towers yet.

#### Outcomes

- Clean rescue: Nat is safe, Jenks and Squiddly adore the party even more, and the Zhentarim know Trollskull is dangerous.
- Amath escapes: she can report that the party is competent and tied to the broken-seal chain.
- Amath captured: she knows Yellowspire serves a higher Zhent master, but she does not know the Stone's location.
- Runner delivered alive: the Zhents try to execute him as a liability; rescuing him can create a scared, useful informant.
- Tube returned: the Zhents still distrust the party if the seal was broken, but the physical evidence leaves play.

## Branch B: The Tube Stays Sealed

Use this if the party hands the runner/tube to Staget, releases Orryn with the tube intact, or refuses to touch the message.

### Harper Hook: The Red Cellar

On Ches 20, a silver raven drops a short note from Remallia:

> A printer's apprentice in the Trades Ward sent a message about red wax, missing charity ledgers, and a cellar that was warmer than it should be. He has not been seen since. Quietly, please.

Mission shape:

- A small print shop or copyist's cellar has been rented through a false charity name.
- The false charity is collecting names, debts, and desperate families, but nothing points cleanly to the Cassalanters yet.
- The cellar contains a half-finished Asmodean rite used to bind a minor devil to destroy records and frighten witnesses.

Encounter for five level-3 PCs:

- 1 spined devil.
- 2 imps, one invisible at the start.
- 2 cultists who flee or surrender quickly.

Clues:

- Red wax stamped with a nine-pointed mark; Religion DC 13 identifies Asmodeus.
- A ledger line: "Founders' Day tithe: children's names confirmed separately."
- A delivery chit for expensive incense paid by "W.C." or "the House of Coin and Smile"; do not include Cassalanter names.
- The missing apprentice is alive, hiding in a coal chute or locked storage nook.

Keep this branch compact. It should feel like the Harpers are feeding the new tavern-listening-post useful work, not like the campaign has swerved away from Fireball.

## Downtime Montage: Ches 20-21

Use quick choices between the branch scene and Fireball.

- Post operating hours. If the party does not choose, Broxley presses for a visible board: noon to midnight is the easy default.
- Staff routines: Lillian mornings/tea, Mosp bar spectacle, Ryba floor lead, Patric chores and lodging, Underbough kitchen.
- Fala checks in about Lif: she has not solved speech yet, but thinks repeated chalkboard prompts and emotionally familiar objects help him focus.
- Frewn spreads a softer rumor that Trollskull had bodies outside on opening night.
- Tally can deliver the urchin bucklers if not already used as the kidnapping hook.
- Rishaal expects borrowed monster books back eventually and may ask whether the party needs something on "gnomes, constructs, or explosive jewelry" after the Fireball.
- Renaer is not staying at Trollskull on Ches 22 unless invited, but Dalakhar believes Trollskull is where he can reach Renaer's rescuers.

## Fireball: Ches 22 Cliffhanger

Use the original adventure's Fireball structure, adjusted to this table's neighborhood.

Default moment: morning of Ches 22, with all PCs in or near Trollskull Manor. The explosion happens outside the front door in Trollskull Alley.

Table-facing ending only:

> Then the room goes completely white. A moment later all of you are knocked to the ground as your newly replaced windows shatter into the Manor. The sound of the explosion is felt more than heard, as all of your ears are set to ringing.
>
> And that's where we'll pick up next week.

Do not reveal cause, victims, number of dead, Dalakhar, Urstul, the Stone, a nimblewright, or Watch conclusions tonight. Let the last image be sensory shock and broken glass.

DM-only facts to preserve for session 15 prep:

- Dalakhar is dead in the blast.
- Two Zhentarim pursuers die nearby.
- Urstul Floxin survives, takes the Stone, and flees toward Gralhund Villa.
- A nimblewright caused the blast using a necklace of fireballs.
- Buildings are damaged but not burning down.
- The City Guard cordons the alley quickly; Barnibus Blastwind and Saeth Cromley arrive later for the formal magical investigation.

## Secrets And Clues To Reveal

- The tube ties the street war to a rock gnome, a stone, Renaer, and Trollskull as a possible contact point.
- The hostile Zhentarim pressure around Yellowspire is likely Manshoon-aligned, not automatically Davil/Yagra's whole faction.
- Dalakhar is moving through Waterdeep before the Fireball and is trying to reach Renaer's rescuers.
- The Xanathar Guild is close enough to the same trail that its gazers and cutters are colliding with Zhentarim couriers.
- The Cassalanter/Asmodean layer should enter as atmosphere or side evidence, not as a direct villa accusation yet.
- The Fireball is not random tavern punishment; Trollskull is where Dalakhar expects help to be.

## Flexible Fallbacks

- If the party spends the whole session on Staget and the tube, end with the Fireball and save retaliation for after the blast.
- If they hand everything to the Watch, Staget can still mention that broken-seal factions may retaliate if the party tampered with evidence.
- If they contact Remallia or Mirt after opening the tube, the Harpers can warn that Yellowspire is probably a trap but cannot move fast enough to rescue Nat without the party.
- If they contact Davil or Yagra, use the Zhent split: they may be alarmed, defensive, or willing to trade information, but they did not order the Yellowspire demand in this prep lane.
- If they skip Yellowspire despite Nat being taken, the Fireball still happens on Ches 22; Nat's rescue becomes a pressure thread after the blast.
- If Yellowspire runs long, have Amath flee early and let the party find one Dalakhar clue, then cut to Ches 22.
