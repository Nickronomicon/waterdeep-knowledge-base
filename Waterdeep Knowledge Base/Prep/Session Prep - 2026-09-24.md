# Session Prep - 2026-09-24

Session 16 starts by rewinding part of [[Sessions/Session 15 - 2026-08-20]] so [[People/PCs/Aladeen]] can play through his Fireball investigation choices instead of inheriting piloted actions. After that, either continue to [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair]] and Captain Fergus, or redirect based on Aladeen's choices and the party's renewed energy.

## Tonight At A Glance

1. **Table reset:** Remind everyone the campaign is at Ches 22, 1492 DR, the same day as the Fireball.
2. **Aladeen spotlight:** Rewind to after [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]] is safe and [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir|Fala's]] death is known.
3. **Retcon gate:** Let Aladeen's actions confirm, replace, or complicate the piloted session 15 witness-gathering.
4. **Rejoin party:** Collapse the replay back into one shared timeline.
5. **Forward motion:** Follow one investigation lead or deploy a response team/encounter if the table needs action.

## Retcon Boundaries

Keep this lightweight and explicit before play starts.

### Keep Unless The Table Strongly Wants Otherwise

- Fala is dead.
- Squiddly survived and is shaken/concussed.
- [[People/NPCs/Dalakhar]] is dead and was coming to meet [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember]].
- A red-caped nimblewright used [[Objects/Broken Necklace of Fireballs]] and searched Dalakhar.
- [[People/PCs/Broethuul]] can still have the broken necklace unless Aladeen's replay directly changes that.
- [[People/PCs/Davy]] can still have Dalakhar's five-gem pouch unless Aladeen's replay directly changes that.
- [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]] can still identify Dalakhar.

### Flexible

- [[People/NPCs/Urstul Floxin]] may remain in Watch custody, slip away, or be handled differently if Aladeen intervenes before Cromley recognizes him.
- Aladeen's witness list and exact questions.
- Whether the party goes directly to [[Places/Sea Maidens Fair]], detours to a different nimblewright owner, or stays near Trollskull.
- What Aladeen tells the Watch, Renaer, Lif, the urchins, Tally, or the party.
- Whether Aladeen notices Broethuul or Davy doing anything suspicious.

### Do Not Force

- Do not force Davy's Crommor reveal through the Davin lead.
- Do not force the party to tell the Watch about Yellowspire.
- Do not make Aladeen responsible for solving the whole Fireball; his spotlight should give him agency, not homework.

## Opening Recap

Use a short, concrete recap:

- The party rescued Nat from Yellowspire, but Amath and Oren escaped.
- The next morning, a Fireball exploded outside Trollskull.
- Squiddly was pulled out from burning debris and healed.
- Fala was recovered from the rubble, already dead.
- The street is full of smoke, broken glass, wounded neighbors, and Watch cordons beginning to form.
- Last session was tired and missing Aladeen, so tonight begins by rewinding to see what Aladeen actually did.

## Scene 1: Aladeen In The Smoke

Start after the rescue turns:

> Squiddly is alive, coughing and confused, with Nat and Jenks clinging to him. Fala's body has just been pulled from the rubble. Tally is staring at them like he has forgotten what his hands are for. Trollskull's front room is becoming a triage room behind you. People are shouting at once. You have maybe ten minutes before the Watch controls the whole scene. What does Aladeen do?

Give Aladeen one clear immediate prompt, then let him choose:

- **Comfort:** Tally, the urchins, Lif, staff, or bystanders.
- **Canvass:** witnesses, rooftops, Frewn, Rishaal, Vincent's client, Martem.
- **Control:** triage room, crowd, Watch arrival, evidence safety.
- **Scheme:** protect Trollskull's reputation, hide/shape evidence, find who benefits.

Run 3 rounds of broad actions. Each action gives a clue, social consequence, or retcon hook.

## Aladeen Leads

Use these as modular NPC cards.

### Tally Fellbranch

State: stunned, grieving, angry at helplessness.

What he gives:

- He saw Fala buried, not the thrower.
- He remembers Fala had been working on Lif notes and probably kept them in the shop or satchel.
- If Aladeen is kind, Tally asks him to make sure Fala is not just counted as one more body.

Useful line:

- "They were helping your ghost. Because of course they were. Because that's what Fala did."

### Nat, Jenks, And Squiddly

State: Nat is fierce and silent, Jenks is scared-talking, Squiddly is woozy.

What they give:

- They did not see the thrower.
- Squiddly remembers "red" and "clanking" only if gently prompted, but this is concussion-fragment unreliable.
- They want to know if Trollskull is still home.

Use:

- Emotional anchor. Let Aladeen be the bard who gives the children a story they can stand inside.

### Lif

State: cannot leave, grief becomes poltergeist pressure.

What he gives:

- Nothing investigative at first.
- If Aladeen speaks to him gently, Lif writes FALA once and then UNDER BAR? or NOTES? as a reminder that Fala left research or correspondence for him.
- If pushed for action, Lif can keep the triage room clean, move water, and help with blankets, but his control is shaky.

Complication:

- On a failed Persuasion/Performance/Religion DC 12, Lif's grief rattles glass and frightens patients. No damage, but Cromley notices the "haunted tavern" problem.

### Emmet Frewn

State: scraped, indignant, eager to blame Trollskull.

What he gives:

- He saw a red shape or "clockwork fop" on a roof, but he wraps it in accusations.
- He will tell the Watch Trollskull brings gang violence unless someone outmaneuvers him socially.

Checks:

- Persuasion DC 13: get the useful witness statement without escalating.
- Intimidation DC 12: he shuts up now, then complains later.
- Performance/Deception DC 14: turn him into a public fool; advances Frewn Countermove later.

### Rishaal The Page-Turner

State: dazed, sitting with book bag, pride injured.

What he gives:

- He saw the construct drop down and search the gnome.
- It took something small enough to vanish in a hand.
- The construct fled east.

Bonus:

- If asked about nimblewrights, Rishaal says the term may apply and suggests the House of Inspired Hands or Watchful Order as better technical sources.

### Vincent Trench And Protected Client

State: Vincent is calm and guarded; client is higher-class and shaken.

What she gives:

- Wood-and-metal man on the roof, no strings.
- Big white or bright hat, carved beard, bright clothes.
- It threw something toward the street just before the blast.

Boundary:

- Vincent asks that she was never here. If Aladeen agrees, he gains Vincent's trust. If he pushes her identity, Vincent shuts down.

### Martem Trec

State: crying near rain barrel, holding or near the broken necklace if Broethuul has not taken it yet.

What he gives:

- He was watching the halfling musicians.
- The construct jumped down, limped, ran past the dead, searched the middle, and left.
- It looked like a sword fighter: hat, feather, red cape, clanking.
- The necklace fell nearby.

Retcon hook:

- If Aladeen reaches Martem before Broethuul, Aladeen can choose whether the necklace goes to Broethuul, the Watch, Aladeen, or the whole party.

### Urstul Floxin

State: badly burned, dazed, trying to leave.

What he gives:

- "Mechanical angel of death" searched the short guy.
- Claims he was shopping for books.
- If soot/clothes shift, a tattoo or criminal vibe can become obvious.

Branch:

- **Custody kept:** Cromley recognizes him and takes him to Staget. This gives Watch/Zhent pressure.
- **Escapes:** Urstul becomes an immediate Manshoonian loose end. He can later appear at Gralhund Villa or send a Zhent team to retrieve what he thinks the party knows.
- **Party detains privately:** risky, illegal, but yields faster Zhent/Stone pressure.

## Likely Investigation Branches

### Branch A: Sea Maidens Fair / Captain Fergus

Start from the current endpoint unless retconned.

Captain Fergus is drunk, performative, and not foolish. He is not Zord, but he knows how to get a message up the chain.

What Fergus knows:

- Zord controls the whole fair.
- The Eyecatcher stays anchored offshore.
- Zord sometimes "appears" aboard other ships without using a boat.
- Nimblewrights were sold through Zord's people, but Fergus claims sales records are "above his wine."

Ways through:

- Persuasion/Performance DC 13: Fergus sends word to Zord.
- Bribe/gift of good wine or spectacle: advantage.
- Intimidation: Fergus laughs, then alerts ship security.
- Sneaking aboard a docked ship: triggers Bregan surveillance, not immediate open combat.

Good endpoint:

- Zord agrees to receive them on the docked ship, in a private tent, or by sending a polished lieutenant.
- End on Zord recognizing the red cape, hat, and beard: "Ah. That one."

### Branch B: House Of Inspired Hands

Use if they want a technical source before Zord.

Reveals:

- Nimblewrights are bespoke constructs, not identical models.
- Nim is not the culprit.
- Zord sold Nim at a strangely low price for such craftsmanship.
- The culprit's red fop styling is not Gondite.

Encounter possibility:

- A frightened apprentice triggers an unstable clockwork display or smoke-belching invention while the party questions Nim. This is nonlethal chaos, good for re-entry after a month.

### Branch C: Davin Crommor

Use if they follow Aladeen's lead or want noble-owner pressure.

Reveals:

- Davin owns a nimblewright guard, but it is not the culprit.
- Davin bought it from Zord to project that House Crommor still matters.
- Davin resents Aladeen and may recognize Jonathan.
- Davy controls whether the scene becomes an identity crisis.

Pressure:

- Davin may call Aladeen "the jester who helped bury my house."
- If Davy remains hidden, let the tension sit; do not force recognition.

### Branch D: Dripping Dagger / Dalakhar Backtrack

Use if they prefer old-school investigation or stall on nimblewright owners.

Reveals:

- Dalakhar stayed one night and left in the morning.
- His room is scrubbed, with a Xanathar death mark on the pillow.
- A Xanathar team may still be watching.

This is the cleanest action encounter if the table needs a fight.

### Branch E: Stay At Trollskull

Use if the table wants grief, consequences, and base management.

Scenes:

- Tally asks what should happen to Fala's shop and notes.
- Lif refuses or fails to write for a while, then writes FALA or HELP THEM.
- Cromley returns with more questions.
- A child from the dead halfling musicians' neighborhood asks who will tell their families.
- Broxley or a guild worker asks whether the tavern is open, closed, or serving relief.

Operational choice:

- Spend 50 gp and two days for window/street repairs.
- Spend 10 gp on relief kitchen to avoid first-tenday Fireball penalty.
- Do nothing or leave without delegating: `-10` to first tenday roll.

### 1. Xanathar Watchers At The Dripping Dagger

Best trigger: party backtracks Dalakhar, asks about Xanathar, or returns to sewer-linked clues.

Team:

- 3 duergar in the room/hall.
- 1 duergar lookout downstairs or across the street.
- Optional gazer if you want a clearer Xanathar signature.

Tactics:

- Start social: "Where is the gnome?"
- If violence starts, two duergar enlarge and one blocks the stairs.
- They prefer capture, intimidation, and escape over murder in public.

Portable clues:

- Black stone Xanathar death mark.
- Note: "If the gnome surfaces, mark the room and wait. Do not chase into Watch light."
- Rumor of Grinda Garloth or Orb Confectioners if you want to seed future Xanathar outpost work.

Scaling:

- Easier: 2 duergar plus lookout.
- Harder: 4 duergar plus gazer, but have them withdraw once bloodied.

### 2. Manshoon Zhent Extraction Or Cleanup

Best trigger: Urstul remains in Watch custody, party talks loudly about Yellowspire, or Aladeen exposes too much about the tube.

Team shapes:

- **Street version:** 1 spy/agent, 3 thugs, 1 flying snake messenger.
- **Harder version:** Agorn-style bard face, 2 thugs, 1 apprentice mage or priestly Banite acolyte.
- **Custody version:** not a fight with the party; the team hits a Watch wagon or station transfer.

Tactics:

- Goal is information control: learn what the party knows, silence Oren/Urstul threads, or recover the tube if visible.
- They open with conversation or a staged accident, not immediate murder.
- If pressed, they use smoke, crowds, and rooftops to withdraw.

Portable clues:

- Black flying-snake token.
- Partial instruction: "F. is in Watch hands. If he speaks, the tower becomes the problem."
- Reference to Yellowspire's failure without naming Manshoon.

### 3. Bregan D'aerthe Soft Intercept

Best trigger: party reaches Sea Maidens Fair, asks too many nimblewright questions, or tries to board a ship.

Team:

- 2 charming sailors/performers as social handlers.
- 1 hidden drow observer using disguise, darkness, or crowd cover.
- Optional gunslinger only as a distant threat, not a level-3 firefight.

Tactics:

- Redirect, charm, test, and report.
- Offer hospitality while quietly separating the party's talkers from sneakers.
- If caught, one handler says Zord loves mysteries and will want to hear this personally.

Portable clues:

- Seven Masks playbill.
- Fine Luskan token or Sea Maidens pass.
- A too-accurate sketch of the party from opening night or opera night.

Encounter mode:

- Social skill challenge, chase through fair tents, or nonlethal scuffle with carnival muscle.

### 4. City Watch Pressure Scene

Best trigger: hidden necklace, Davy's gemstone witness, conflicting Aladeen retcon, or party tries to flee Watch questioning.

Team:

- Cromley plus 4 Watch constables.
- Barnibus if magic/evidence is central.

Tactics:

- Legal pressure, not combat.
- Cromley asks direct questions and watches who answers too quickly.
- Barnibus can detect magical residue or identify fireball beads if the necklace appears.

Consequences:

- Cooperation earns "witness" status and a later call-in.
- Contradictions make Trollskull a legal headache.
- If they surrender the necklace, the Watch inventories it; if they conceal it, hidden-evidence clock starts.

### 5. Trollskull Opportunists

Best trigger: party stays at Trollskull, leaves it unattended, or table needs a lower-stakes fight.

Team:

- 4-6 desperate looters, pickpockets, or thrill-seeking toughs.
- One has a fake Watch sash or claims to be "collecting evidence."

Tactics:

- They try to steal from damaged Corellon's Crown, dead bystanders, or Trollskull's broken front.
- They flee quickly when faced with real force.

Portable clues:

- They heard "the gnome had gems."
- One saw Davy take the pouch and is testing whether that means looting is allowed.
- Can become a Davy gemstone consequence without involving major factions.

### 6. Frewn's Bad Decision

Best trigger: Aladeen humiliates Frewn or Trollskull turns relief work into good press.

Team:

- Frewn plus 2 hired complaint-makers or "concerned citizens."
- No combat unless the party escalates.

Tactics:

- Spread the line that Trollskull brings ghosts, gangs, and explosions.
- File guild complaints about unsafe premises and undead service.
- Try to poach frightened staff or customers.

Counterplay:

- Aladeen can obliterate this socially with Performance/Persuasion.
- Let the win be public and satisfying, but advance Frewn Countermove later.

## Clue Safety Net

If the party stalls, give one of these through the nearest reasonable source:

- Rishaal: "Nimblewrights are fashionable nonsense with very expensive owners. Start with Gond or the Watchful Order."
- Vincent: "Your puppet came from money. Cheap killers do not wear custom clockwork faces."
- Cromley: "The gnome was running to your door. Someone knew exactly when he would arrive."
- Renaer: "Dalakhar said he carried something for my father. I do not know what, but I know my father: if someone killed for it, it was real."
- Sea Maidens worker: "Zord sold clockwork men all spring. Ask a captain if you want to be laughed at politely."
- Harper whisper: "Crommor quietly bought one. Very loudly."

## Secrets To Reveal

- The nimblewright searched Dalakhar because Dalakhar had something worth stealing.
- The stolen thing is almost certainly the same "Stone" from the tube message, but you can let the players say that first.
- Zord is the common seller for the city's nimblewrights.
- The guilty nimblewright's exact costume is unique enough to identify the buyer.
- Urstul being in Watch custody is unstable; someone will want him silenced or recovered.
- Fala's death creates a neighborhood wound, not just a body count.
- Lif's grief can become a new kind of haunting pressure.

## Avoid Tonight

- Do not dump the full Grand Game explanation.
- Do not force Gralhund Villa before the party has chosen a route.
- Do not turn Zord hostile too early; he is more useful as charming danger.
- Do not spend too long adjudicating tavern accounting before the table has momentum.
- Do not punish Aladeen for retconning piloted actions; use his choices to sharpen the timeline.

- **Mystery ending:** Zord or another owner identifies the culprit's buyer.
- **Personal ending:** Davin Crommor's door opens.
- **Home ending:** Lif writes FALA on the broken chalkboard, then HELP THEM.
