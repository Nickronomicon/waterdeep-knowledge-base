**THE SCORE**

Identify who bought the nimblewright that attacked Trollskull.

Agree on four things:
1. How do we reach and enter the ship?
2. Who gets to the book, and what do the others do?
3. What do we do if challenged or discovered?
4. How do we leave and reunite?

**ONE PREPARATION EACH**

Name one skill, tool, ability, or spell you are using to prepare. When an obstacle makes it relevant, explain what you did beforehand and resolve it with the DM then.

You can prepare contacts, forged papers, a practiced climb, transport, a distraction, or something else plausible. Actual costs and resources still apply. Preparation cannot grant an ability you do not have or erase an event that already happened.

You don't need a perfect plan. Choose the approach, assign jobs, and keep an exit.
