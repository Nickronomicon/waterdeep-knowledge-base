**BACK TO WATERDEEP — CHES 23**

Yesterday, a nimblewright threw the Fireball that killed Fala and Dalakhar outside Trollskull. Witnesses saw it search the gnome and flee: red cape, broad bright hat, bright clothing, carved beard.

The Stone of Golorr may have been what it took. The Cassalanters say the Stone leads to Neverember's hidden 500,000 dragons. You agreed to help save their twins; their proposed ritual is Tarsahk 9, two days before the birthday. Your reward remains conditional.

Remallia traced eight known Luskan nimblewrights to Zardoz Zord. Trench returned your money rather than investigate him, naming Menzoberranzan as the reason.

This morning, Margo wrote you an introduction after two duergar and a gazer interrupted her rehearsal. Their orders wanted one of you alive for information about the gnome and Stone.

Zord refused Aladeen and Elior's questions. Elior threatened his reputation; the audience ended. But he noticed Zord repeatedly glance at a bound book on his desk whenever customers came up. Probably the ledger.

You are back at the fair's pier. The next score is the buyer's identity. You discussed asking your bronze-dragon acquaintance Zelifarn for help; no plan has been committed yet.
