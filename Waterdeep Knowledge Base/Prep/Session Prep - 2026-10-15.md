# Session Prep - 2026-10-15

**Draft proposals, not established events.** Build tonight around one answer: **who owns the nimblewright that searched Dalakhar?** Margo can supply access, Zord can supply ownership, and a Xanathar response can make the investigation dangerous. Success is a usable next target or a concrete plan to obtain the sales ledger.

## Five-Minute Setup

- [ ] Open on Ches 23 as requested in the Session 18 notes; confirm the overnight long rest/resources with players.
- [ ] Send the Cassalanter answer; acknowledge it briefly and move to the investigation.
- [ ] Put Margo at a late-morning rehearsal at the Smiling Siren. Give her location through staff without a search roll.
- [ ] Choose Margo's relationship to Zord below. Recommended: occasional guest and social acquaintance.
- [ ] Load two **legacy duergar** and one **gazer**, plus a theater or warehouse map.
- [ ] Have the Eyecatcher map available if players choose surveillance; the ledger is in J30.
- [ ] End with Gralhunds identified, or a specific ledger operation ready to run.

## Opening Recap And Party State

Recap aloud:

> Yesterday's killer was a nimblewright. It searched Dalakhar's body before fleeing, making its owner your best lead to the Stone of Golorr and Neverember's gold. Remallia traced all eight known Luskan models to Zardoz Zord. Trench returned your money rather than investigate him. You have a lead on his opera companion, Margo, and owe the Cassalanters an answer this morning.

- Five level-3 PCs: bard, blood hunter, rogue, sorcerer, paladin. Sheets are older exports; confirm actual resources after the overnight transition.
- Davy supports helping the children while reserving judgment over the hoard's disposition. Acceptance need not erase that distinction.
- No buyer list, record location, floorplan, surveillance, or prep declarations have been obtained.
- The fair visit was retconned. Ship details must be learned in play.
- Trench disclosed a Menzoberranzan connection; Jarlaxle/Bregan identities remain unrevealed.
- Broethuul's necklace and Amberleaf Dispatch remain private. Davy's gems are party-known.
- The birthday is roughly twenty days away in dialogue; resolve calendar discrepancies before setting an absolute deadline.

## Three Possible Directions

| Direction | Core Scene | Useful Outcome | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Margo opens the door — recommended** | Rehearsal conversation, optional Xanathar interception, introduction to Zord | A meeting, or a firsthand description of where Zord works | Gives the party's chosen lead a payoff; Margo need not know any villain secrets |
| **A sales visit becomes reconnaissance** | Elior poses as a prospective buyer; others examine access and staff routines | Customer reference, ledger sighting, or confirmed record location | Cover can draw Zord's interest; run straight conversation if they tell the truth |
| **One owner, then a decision** | Check an existing owner such as the House of Inspired Hands | Distinct appearance rules it out; sales paperwork confirms the seller keeps records | Useful confirmation, but montage further eliminations once the party understands the pattern |

These are options rather than mandatory sequential scenes. Do not make the party investigate eight innocent owners before it can advance.

## Margo At Rehearsal

**Purpose:** reward the party's chosen inquiry with one actionable fact and one choice.

Opening image: Margo stops the accompanist for playing too delicately while stagehands wrestle a painted sea monster into position. She recognizes Elior as the young noble with the opera joke.

### Relationship Choice

The original adventure makes Margo a Fair performer and intimate guest aboard the Eyecatcher; the Remix also places her there. Those facts are not table truth. In particular, the published version knows secrets about Jarlaxle that need not transfer into this campaign.

**Recommended adaptation matching the Session 18 notes:** Zord cultivated Margo as a local celebrity; she has visited his ship as a guest but is not his confidante. Her opera attendance was personal. She can know his habits and a room's appearance without knowing his identity, surveillance system, or buyers.

If you prefer the raw note's strictest version, keep her entirely ashore: she offers an introduction and a description of his social tastes. Obtain the ledger/location clue from a clerk, crew member, or surveillance instead.

### Offer Two Useful Things

1. **Social access:** she will write an introduction or arrange an ordinary meeting. Zord enjoys being interesting and learning other people's business. This does not grant unescorted ship access.
2. **Concrete information, if using the guest adaptation:** she saw a bound sales ledger at the desk in his private cabin on the Eyecatcher while he dealt with business. She can sketch that cabin and the route she was escorted along. She cannot map the whole ship or promise current guard positions.

Suggested line, original dialogue:

> “He likes an audience. If you want something from him, give him a story worth hearing. If you want his paperwork, that is a rather different invitation.”

Her condition: do not implicate her in theft or use her name as permission to wander his ship. She can decline to help rob an acquaintance while still discussing an atrocity investigation.

**Checks:** no roll to receive the introduction or basic observations once they explain a reasonable purpose. Persuasion might earn her escort; Insight clarifies that she is protecting her social standing rather than obviously confessing to a conspiracy. Failure costs a favor, time, or discretion, rather than removing the only clue.

## Zord: Talk Or Take The Ledger

### Honest Conversation

Follow Remix Part 5C: a meeting is obtainable. Zord asks why they want a particular construct's owner. Once they give him a useful account, he checks his information and identifies **House Gralhund**, with its address.

**The cost is information:** Zord learns about the Stone investigation and can act on it. Do not require the party to invent a perfect cover or promise a theft when honest disclosure satisfies his curiosity.

### Prospective-Buyer Cover

Let Elior's proposed gift purchase work as a plausible opening. Zord asks about intended duties and preferences. A reference to a known, innocent customer is easy to give; extracting every private buyer is a bigger request. Crew inquiries or observing paperwork can establish the real target even if the sales pitch falters.

### Ledger Operation

**DM fact:** Remix Parts 4B/5C put the Ledger of Nimblewright Sales at the desk in Zord's cabin, **Eyecatcher J30**. Establish this in play through firsthand observation, a credible contact, or surveillance. A meeting on a docked ship does not relocate the ledger there.

Before calling for prep declarations, the table should be able to state:

- Score: copy the sales entries and identifying descriptions; taking the book is optional.
- Place: Eyecatcher, Zord's cabin.
- Access: a discovered invitation, supply route, boat approach, or other player plan.
- Missing information: one concrete gap, such as cabin access or a patrol routine.
- Exit: how they leave with the information.

Use the established five-phase/flashback procedure in [[Prep/Session Prep - 2026-10-01]]. A plausible plan and one useful surveillance scene are enough to begin; avoid demanding an exhaustive security audit.

**Eight/nine boundary:** the Remix has nine sold nimblewrights; table truth is eight **known** Luskan models. Prep the campaign's complete buyer handout before showing it. An additional unregistered Gralhund purchase is an available explanation, not an automatic reveal. Likewise, Davin Crommor is an established campaign owner and must be accounted for. Do not blindly hand out the published roster.

After a successful ledger score, use bespoke appearance details or delivery correspondence to make the Gralhund entry actionable. The players should not need another round of random household visits to locate the relevant entry.

## Vajra's Follow-Up

Use once if the party requests help or spends roughly 15 minutes debating without identifying a next action. Jonathan is an established Sending recipient. Suggested 25-word Sending:

> “Need progress on the construct. Seller's records or an honest conversation may identify its owner. Tell me your next step; I can arrange an introduction.”

- She knows their prior Fireball report and the Neverember concern. Have them report Remallia's new sales information before she discusses that detail.
- Her help buys access: a contact, appointment, or legitimate inquiry. She does not already know the guilty owner.
- If contacted about a known owner, she can arrange one check and help consolidate findings.
- If they are already acting, withhold the nudge. She does not assign a separate urgent side mission tonight.

## Combat: The Wrong Audience

**Recommended encounter:** two duergar Xanathar operatives and one gazer interrupt the Smiling Siren rehearsal. This is a smaller, campaign-specific adaptation of the Remix's duergar response teams, not the full published team.

### Trigger And Stakes

A Xanathar tail follows the party after their renewed public inquiries; let a PC spot the repeated face or a floating eye at an upper window. The operatives have heard that the tavern owners are following the dead gnome's trail. They want the party alive for questioning and suspect it knows where the Stone went. They do not know the Cassalanter deal, private evidence, or anything the party has only discussed behind closed doors.

Deploy after the Margo conversation has delivered its useful information. Margo is not another kidnapped quest giver. She and the accompanist withdraw through a rear door while the party chooses how to respond.

Demand, original dialogue:

> “You keep asking about the dead gnome. Our employer has questions too. Come quietly, and the singer finishes her rehearsal.”

Negotiation, deception, or slipping away can avert the fight. If they do, keep the combat available later only when a surviving tail or renewed faction contact plausibly supports it.

### Map And Interactive Features

Use a room about 50 by 40 feet: front entrance, rear backstage exit, 5-foot-high stage, tables in the middle, and an accessible 10-foot-high lighting gallery. Place one clearly marked scenery-release rope beside the stage.

**Optional encounter rulings; explain them when players inspect the features:**

| Feature | Table Use |
|---|---|
| Rope and suspended canvas sea monster | An adjacent creature can use an action to release it. Creatures in its marked 10-foot-square landing area make DC 12 Dexterity saves or take 1d6 bludgeoning and fall prone. The fallen frame becomes difficult terrain. One use. |
| Heavy velvet curtain | Drawing it with an action blocks sight across the stage, including eye-ray targeting. It provides no physical damage protection. |
| Tables and overturned scenery | Use ordinary cover; tipping a table is a simple object interaction. |
| Gallery | Reached by ladder or a climbing approach. Gives the rogue a second route and the gazer an exposed perch. |

Both sides can use the scenery. Telegraph the suspended weight; avoid an unannounced trap or another fire threatening civilians.

### Enemy Turns

- **First duergar:** starts visible and spends its first action enlarging. Its growing silhouette against the painted sea monster is the opening spectacle. It then blocks the obvious exit or grapples an isolated PC to take them alive.
- **Second duergar:** used invisibility before entry; scrape of boots or moving curtain provides a warning. It becomes visible when it attacks **or enlarges**, as the legacy trait specifies. It cannot enlarge, turn invisible, and attack in one turn.
- **Gazer:** flies near the gallery and fires its normal two random, distinct eye rays. Roll the rays; do not select telekinesis every round. If that ray comes up, it can shove a PC or operate an unattended light object such as the scenery latch. It cannot pluck worn/carried evidence from a PC.
- **Morale:** after one duergar falls, the other bargains or tries to withdraw. The gazer tries to escape after round three or once both handlers are beaten. No enemy executes a downed PC; the assignment is capture.

Xanathar can potentially observe through the gazer already. Killing it ends this surveillance opportunity; it does not erase information already seen. Its escape preserves an ongoing watcher, rather than being the only way a report could reach the Guild.

### Difficulty And Adjustment

For **five rested level-3 PCs using legacy 2014 encounter math**, two CR-1 duergar plus one CR-1/2 gazer total **500 base XP / 1,000 adjusted XP**, between the party's medium (750) and hard (1,125) thresholds. Environmental control and the sorcerer's low sheet AC can make it swingier than the budget suggests.

- Easier: one duergar plus the gazer, or keep the gazer observing rather than attacking.
- Harder: start with three duergar and one gazer, **700 base / 1,400 adjusted XP**. Use only if current sheets/resources support it; choose before initiative rather than silently adding waves.
- Keep ordinary stat blocks and action costs. Review the actual Foundry versions; newer duergar blocks can have different abilities.
- Give all five PCs room to contribute: frontline containment, a flank through scenery, spell control over sight lines, and interaction with the rigging.

### Clue And Consequences

An operative carries a short original order:

> “The Trollskull crew are following the gnome. Bring one in breathing. Find out whether they have his stone or know who took it.”

This reveals a rival's uncertainty, rather than granting Xanathar omniscience. A prisoner can identify the gazer as Guild surveillance and admit they were following the PCs. They cannot name the nimblewright's buyer.

For a new outpost lead, optionally adapt the Remix's equipment directive to Grinda Garloth, but keep it background unless the party chooses it. The main clue from Margo should already be safe in the players' hands.

Afterward, Watch takes a brief statement; visible aggressors and Margo's testimony support the party's account. This encounter does not erase the unresolved wagon-ambush investigation. Margo's thanks can improve access to Zord, but the fight is not a prerequisite for her basic clue.

### Duergar Loot

**For the whole encounter:** the capture orders above, **one potion of healing** (2d4 + 2 HP), and **two different results from the d6 table below**, distributed between the duergar. Roll twice, rerolling duplicates, or choose what suits the party. A third duergar adds ordinary equipment and a personal keepsake rather than another treasure roll.

**Recommended selection tonight:** the smoke pellet and tap-stones, plus the potion. These give the party tools to experiment with during a ship operation. This is a proposed reward package for five level-3 PCs, not recovered inventory yet.

All named items below are original encounter loot. Only the tap-stones are a permanent magic item; the salve and wax are single-use homebrew preparations. Mundane gear uses ordinary equipment rules. Make the consumables' effects clear when identified or demonstrated; do not require expenditure just to discover what they do.

| d6 | Find | Description And Table Use |

**Personal detail, free:** one duergar carries an unfinished miniature of an Underdark mushroom garden, whittled from soapstone. It has no magical effect or required plot connection; it is something someone spent time making.

A pellet or potion used during combat is expended; report the actual remaining loot. Duergar enlargement and invisibility are innate abilities, so this equipment does not grant those powers.

Keep the capture orders available independently of random treasure. Any Grinda equipment directive remains an optional separate clue, not a prerequisite for using the recovered gear.

## If They Choose A Different Route

- **Direct to the docks:** use the social or sales scene immediately. The same response team can intercept them at a rigging warehouse only if a Xanathar tail actually follows them there. Replace theater scenery with a suspended sail bundle.
- **Visit known owners:** let the first distinctive construct be seen and accounted for. Sales papers or the owner's account point to Zord's records. Montage repetitive eliminations; preserve Davy's choice about approaching Davin Crommor.
- **Investigate Lif/Fala:** allow a brief sincere scene or arrange a later Dorgar visit. Do not fabricate Fala's ghost to redirect them. Close with the party choosing its next investigation action.
- **Investigate the Cassalanter claims:** honor it. A clerical appointment or request for the cited text is meaningful progress. Use the unresolved clerical discrepancy honestly; do not confirm the family's story by fiat.
- **Already obtain the Gralhund clue:** stop preparing the Zord heist. Let them observe Gralhund Villa and close on the situation there; consult the Remix before scheduling its raid rather than triggering it solely because a session clock expired.

## Background Agendas And End Beats

- **Zord:** trade information, learn why the party cares, protect his records. Publicly show interest only after he or his crew learns of the inquiry.
- **Xanathar:** turn accumulated attention into a targeted capture/surveillance attempt.
- **Cassalanters:** acknowledge the answer and maintain the rescue pressure without demanding another full villa scene.
- **Vajra:** get a useful report and facilitate a concrete next step.
- **Watch:** unresolved wagon statements remain pending. Use a short request to report later rather than another procedural opening scene.
- **Trollskull:** Patric covers drinks, Lif is silent, windows need repairs. Delegate routine business to staff if players wish; do not resolve costs without a decision.

Any of these is a successful ending:

1. “The Gralhunds bought it. Here is their address.”
2. “The ledger is in that cabin; this is how we get aboard and out.”
3. The party has earned a Zord appointment and made one meaningful access/security discovery.
