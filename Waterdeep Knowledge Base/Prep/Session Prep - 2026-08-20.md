# Session Prep - 2026-08-20

Session 15 begins seconds after the Fireball outside [[Places/Trollskull Manor]]. The opening is a rescue under pressure, followed by a short crime-scene window, Watch control, [[People/NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer's]] explanation of Dalakhar, and the first leg of the Remix nimblewright investigation.

## Tonight At A Glance

1. **Rescue:** Save [[People/NPCs/Squiddly]] before burning debris collapses. Recover [[People/NPCs/Fala Lefaliir|Fala]] and learn they are dead.
2. **Crime scene:** Let the party question [[People/NPCs/Urstul Floxin|Urstul]], inspect Dalakhar, and gather witness accounts before the cordon closes.
3. **Authorities:** Barnibus Blastwind and Saeth Cromley take control. Renaer arrives and identifies Dalakhar.
4. **Investigation:** Identify a nimblewright, find at least two owners, and learn that Captain [[People/NPCs/Zardoz Zord|Zord]] sold all of them.
5. **Likely endpoint:** The party chooses between questioning Zord, investigating an owner such as Davin Crommor, or backtracking Dalakhar to the Inn of the Dripping Dagger.

## Target End State

- Squiddly's fate is resolved by player action.
- Fala's death lands as a personal loss and a neighborhood consequence.
- The party knows a distinct red-clad nimblewright caused the blast and searched Dalakhar's body.
- The party knows Dalakhar was coming to Trollskull to meet Renaer with something valuable tied to Dagult Neverember.
- The party has at least two owner leads and can connect nimblewright sales to Zord without depending on one check.
- The Gralhunds remain a discoverable conclusion, not an automatic Watch exposition dump.

## Remix Continuity Guardrails

- The **Gralhund nimblewright** threw the fireball bead, searched Dalakhar, took the [[Objects/Stone of Golorr]], and returned to Gralhund Villa.
- Urstul Floxin survived but **did not take the Stone**. He remains wounded onsite briefly, then slips away unless stopped.
- The House of Inspired Hands owns Nim, one of Zord's sold nimblewrights. Nim did **not** build the culprit and has **no nimblewright detector**.
- All Waterdeep nimblewrights are bespoke. Shared construction does not make them visually identical.
- The red robe, foppish feathered hat, and stylized Van Dyke beard uniquely identify the Gralhund construct.
- These facts supersede the unused DM-only Urstul/Stone line in the prior Session 14 prep and the contradictory original-adventure investigation path.

## Opening Recap

- On Ches 19, Trollskull opened under the Skullduggers sign and a Zhentarim-Xanathar clash spilled into the alley.
- The party opened a flying-snake-sealed message about a living gnome, the Stone, missing eyes, old Neverember channels, Xanathar watchers, and Renaer's new house.
- On Ches 20, hostile Zhentarim kidnapped Nat. The party found Oren, attacked Yellowspire, rescued Nat, and left both Oren and Amath Sercent alive in the wind.
- Mid-morning on Ches 22, white light filled Trollskull, the new windows shattered inward, and the blast threw everyone down.

## Scene 1: Three Heartbeats After The Blast

Open with the ringing in their ears, smoke moving past the broken windows, and Lif's chalkboard slamming against the bar. Outside, Nat and Jenks are screaming Squiddly's name while Tally and two apprentices claw at fallen masonry beside Corellon's Crown.

Tell the players all three immediate facts before asking for actions:

- Squiddly is pinned beneath a burning awning and shattered cart, motionless.
- A green sleeve and one hand are visible beneath rubble near Fala's storefront; Tally believes Fala is alive.
- Other survivors are screaming for help around the blast center.

### Crisis Turns

Run three loose crisis turns. Each PC gets one meaningful action per turn; initiative is unnecessary unless the table wants it. State at the end of each turn that the fire is spreading and the wreckage over Squiddly is shifting.

**Squiddly dies at the end of turn 3 unless the party both frees him and stabilizes or heals him.** Extinguishing or containing the fire buys one additional turn. Magical healing before extraction keeps him alive for one extra turn but does not remove the collapsing-debris deadline.

| Need | Example Resolution |
|---|---|
| Reach or protect Squiddly | Athletics, Acrobatics, or Survival DC 12; a fitting spell or clever tool succeeds without a roll. |
| Lift or dismantle debris | Athletics DC 13 or carpenter's/thieves' tools DC 12; a lever, coordinated lift, or strong magic grants advantage or succeeds. |
| Stabilize him | Medicine DC 10 or any magical healing after a path is opened. |
| Control the fire | Survival or Arcana DC 12, water, cold magic, or smothering material; success adds turn 4. |

On a failed rescue action, inflict a complication rather than closing the path: `1d6` fire or bludgeoning damage, lost gear, or an endangered helper. Nat and Jenks can Help if directed, but keeping them out of the collapse zone should not cost a PC's action.

### Fala And Tally

Fala is already dead. Recovering the body requires two useful actions or successes to clear and shore the rubble. There is no deadline, and failed rolls cost time rather than changing the outcome.

- A successful Medicine check can establish death as soon as Fala's head and chest are accessible.
- Tally keeps working until physically stopped. Once the body is free, he becomes very quiet and asks someone else to close Fala's eyes.
- Fala's satchel contains ordinary herbs, two healer's kits, and the notes from their unfinished research into helping [[People/NPCs/Lif]]. Do not turn the death reveal into a loot scene; Tally safeguards the satchel unless the party specifically preserves it for Lif.
- Do not volunteer resurrection logistics tonight. If the players pursue them, a temple can preserve the body while cost, consent, and availability become a later decision.

### Lif And Staff

- Lif cannot leave the manor. He throws the front door open, points through the window with chalk, slides water and blankets toward the threshold, and catches falling interior debris.
- Once Fala's death is brought inside, Lif's chalk snaps. Bottles rattle once behind the bar and then stop.
- Lillian organizes blankets and names; Mosp carries the injured; Ryba clears spectators; Patric runs for the Watch or a temple; Corvin and Miri turn the taproom into a triage room.
- A PC who takes command with Medicine or Persuasion DC 12 stabilizes the general response and earns Barnibus/Cromley's respect later. Failure still produces help, but the crowd contaminates more of the scene.

## Casualty Baseline

The blast kills eleven unless Squiddly also dies:

- Fala Lefaliir.
- Dalakhar, a male rock gnome.
- Two male Zhentarim sellswords with black flying-snake tattoos.
- Two human servants and one half-elf servant running North Ward errands.
- Four halfling street musicians.

Eight other bystanders have burns, cuts, or crush injuries. The fireball itself did not ignite the buildings; the small fires come from shattered awnings, a brazier, lamp oil, and a wrecked cart.

## Scene 2: The Five-Minute Crime Scene

### Running Timeline

| Time After Blast | Event |
|---:|---|
| 0-3 minutes | Rescue turns, first witness statements, Urstul attempts to orient himself. |
| 3-5 minutes | Urstul slips away if no one has detained or closely watched him. |
| About 5 minutes | City Guard cordons both ends of the alley and protects the bodies. |
| About 20 minutes | Barnibus Blastwind, Saeth Cromley, and the formal Watch detail arrive. |
| About 25 minutes | Renaer arrives, alarmed and expecting to meet Dalakhar. |

Do not run the clock so tightly that helping Squiddly costs all investigation. A PC actively scanning while others rescue can find Urstul or Dalakhar; witnesses remain available after the Watch arrives.

### Dalakhar's Body

The dead gnome lies near the blast center. His cloak and boots carry dry sewer waste. His dagger remains in hand, but his clothes and pockets were disturbed after the blast.

- Investigation DC 12: someone searched him after he fell; the soot and fabric folds have been recently smeared.
- Perception DC 13: two nearby corpses bear visible black flying-snake tattoos and were moving toward him when they died.
- The gnome carries a pouch with five 100 gp gemstones. Taking it unseen requires Sleight of Hand DC 13. On failure, the character still gets it, but a witness tells the Watch.
- He has no Stone, message, or identifying paper.
- Searching openly is not itself theft, but removing possessions before the Watch arrives creates legal and social risk.

### Urstul Floxin

Urstul is badly burned, disoriented, and trying to look like an unlucky shopper. If questioned:

- He gives his real name without thinking.
- He claims he was going to Book Wyrm's Treasure and remembers only white light and heat.
- Insight DC 13: he is withholding most of why he was here.
- If pressed, he says he saw a "mechanical angel of death" move among the bodies, bend over the gnome, and run east.
- His black flying-snake tattoo is on his left breast and is not visible without treatment, searching, or stripped armor.

If healed, he is grateful only as long as needed to leave. If detained, let that deviation stand: the Watch takes him, Manshoon's people begin planning extraction, and the party has earned a much more direct Zhentarim lead.

### Witness Matrix

No check is required to obtain what a willing witness actually saw. Checks locate witnesses quickly, calm them, or catch contradictions.

| Witness | What They Saw | Key Use |
|---|---|---|
| Jezrynne Hornraven | A wood-and-copper figure on a roof threw a bead into the street. Red robe, feathered red hat, long pointed beard. | Establishes attacker and full description. |
| Martem Trec | Hid behind a rain barrel; a broken necklace with two fireball beads fell nearby after the construct fled. | Physical evidence and rooftop route. |
| [[People/NPCs/Rishaal the Page-Turner]] | After the blast, the construct dropped down, searched the gnome, took something, and ran east between two buildings. | Establishes theft and target. |
| [[People/NPCs/Emmet Frewn]] | Saw a red-clad clockwork shape leap from above; also loudly blames Trollskull for bringing violence to the alley. | Redundant construct clue with social pressure. |
| Urstul Floxin | Claims a mechanical angel moved among the bodies. | Redundant body-search clue and future recognition. |

If the party keeps the necklace, concealing material evidence can bring up to a 200 gp fine and as much as a tenday of hard labor. Barnibus will inventory it if it is surrendered.

## Revelation List

| Revelation | At Least Three Paths |
|---|---|
| A nimblewright caused the blast | Jezrynne saw it throw the bead; Martem found its necklace; Frewn saw it on the roof; Arcana identifies witness descriptions. |
| Dalakhar was the intended target | Rishaal saw the body searched; Barnibus reconstructs the pursuit; Urstul was following him; the body lies at the blast center. |
| The attacker stole something | Rishaal saw the search; soot and clothing show rifled pockets; Urstul describes the mechanical figure among bodies. |
| Dalakhar was coming to Trollskull | Renaer arranged the meeting; Barnibus sees the pursuit headed toward the tavern; Dalakhar's route through the alley ends at its door. |
| Zord supplied the city's nimblewrights | Any cooperative owner names him; Luskan/Sea Maidens research points to him; the House of Inspired Hands names him. |
| The guilty construct belongs to the Gralhunds | Zord identifies the unique beard/hat model; stolen sales records list it; records of witness aboard the Scarlet Marpenoth show its service. |

## Scene 3: The Watch Takes Over

Barnibus Blastwind is the magical investigator: precise, private, and unwilling to deputize adventurers. Saeth Cromley is the veteran sergeant who controls constables, witnesses, and the cordon.

They establish privately that Dalakhar was running from three armed pursuers toward Trollskull and that none of them expected the blast. They question the party about:

- Whether they knew the gnome or expected a visitor.
- What they saw before and after the explosion.
- Recent threats, gang violence, and magical enemies around Trollskull.
- Any missing evidence or possessions removed from bodies.
- The party's magical ability to create an explosion like this.

Useful rulings:

- Honest cooperation means the Watch treats them as witnesses and victims despite prior Trollskull incidents.
- Persuasion DC 15 gets Barnibus to share his pursuit reconstruction.
- A lie about the necklace, body search, or Yellowspire becomes serious only if contradicted by evidence or witnesses.
- Barnibus refuses to make them official investigators, but Cromley cannot stop residents from talking to their neighbors after statements are taken.
- If the party reports Yellowspire, the Watch's attention divides between the fireball and a Banite murder scene; this improves credibility but raises City Watch Attention to full.

Barnibus lines:

- "I have not accused you. I am trying to preserve that state of affairs."
- "The dead were moving toward your door. That makes you relevant, not necessarily responsible."
- "Trust the Watch, or at minimum refrain from reorganizing my evidence."

Cromley lines:

- "First the wounded. Then the witnesses. Then everyone gets clever."
- "Nobody leaves with a corpse's pockets heavier than they found them."

## Renaer And Dalakhar

Renaer arrives after the formal Watch detail, expecting Dalakhar and immediately recognizing the body.

He tells the party:

- Dalakhar was an agent of Dagult Neverember and had been assigned to watch Renaer.
- Dalakhar disappeared a few weeks ago, shortly before Renaer's kidnapping.
- Dalakhar sent an urgent message asking to meet. Renaer selected Trollskull as a place he trusted.
- The message said Dalakhar carried something valuable for Dagult, could not deliver it, and hoped Renaer could help.
- Renaer does not know what the item was.

Renaer recognizes the session 14 tube message as likely describing Dalakhar. He is shaken that the warning about "the lord's son's new house" led directly to Trollskull and angry that his father's shadow has killed Fala and endangered the children.

## Scene 4: On The Matter Of Nimblewrights

Once the party has the description, every plausible research method should produce one or two owners. Checks determine speed and breadth, not whether the investigation continues.

### Identifying The Construct

- Arcana DC 13: a nimblewright, built from Luskan technomancy based on old Calishite designs.
- Arcana DC 17: Bowgentra Summertaen of the Watchful Order owns one.
- Investigation DC 13: two known owners. Add one owner for every 2 points above the DC.
- Following the Sea Maidens/Luskan angle directly also points to Zord.
- A Harper, Gray Hands, Rishaal, or Vincent Trench inquiry returns the House of Inspired Hands plus Davin Crommor.

### First Owner Leads

Prefer these two so the session offers both campaign plot and personal pressure:

1. **House of Inspired Hands:** A witness remembers a similar construct in the Twin Parades as part of Gond's procession.
2. **Davin Crommor:** Alley gossip or faction research recalls the scowling clockwork guard at the diminished Crommor villa.

Optional additional owners:

- Bowgentra Summertaen: featureless head, used as majordomo at the Watchful Order guildhall.
- Lord Corin Dezlentyr: green gemstone right eye, dressed as a swashbuckler.
- Mother Tamra's House of Graces: eight intersecting metal halos, used as a housecleaner.
- City Armory: extra plates resemble breastplate and greaves, used as a fencing partner.
- House of Wonder: feminine form, white robe, silver bob; its hidden clairvoyance crystal has been removed.

Every owner knows the same core facts: Captain Zord sold the construct, the price was a surprisingly low 25,000 dragons, and each model was customized.

### House Of Inspired Hands

Nim has layered metal-feather hair and assisted in the Twin Parades. Nim communicates nonverbally with Valetta's help.

- Nim was purchased from Zord and recognizes the culprit description as another Luskan-built nimblewright.
- Valetta can name Zord and the Sea Maidens Faire's rented pier.
- Nim did not build the culprit and does not have a detector.
- If examined for several hours, Nim's deeply hidden clairvoyance crystal can be discovered with Arcana DC 18. Detect magic plus Arcana DC 15 can trace its attunement toward the Eyecatcher in the harbor. This is bonus information, not needed tonight.

### Davin Crommor

Davin's nimblewright has black onyx eyes, a permanent rictus scowl, and a House Crommor tabard. It guards the front hall of the family's diminished but still respectable villa. It does **not** have the culprit's beard or red costume.

Davin bought it from Zord because a conspicuous construct signals that House Crommor is not finished. He resents questions that make his household look implicated but will name Zord to redirect scrutiny.

Personal beats:

- Give Hannah a private cue that Davy recognizes the villa, Davin, and the construct immediately. Ask what Davy does; do not call for a recognition check.
- Davin recognizes Aladeen as the creator of the Crommor Collapse and receives him with cold fury.
- Davin recognizes Jonathan and the abandoned Eagleshield-Crommor understanding.
- Davin does not automatically recognize Davy. Let recognition happen only if Davy exposes herself, the disguise is put under sustained scrutiny, or Hannah invites the risk.
- Do not use this scene to force Davy's identity reveal. Its purpose is to put the choice in her hands.

## Scene 5: The Zord Decision

Getting an appointment with Zord is easy. If the party describes the culprit and explains enough to satisfy his curiosity, he excuses himself, checks his records, and returns with the Gralhund name and address.

Zord's goals:

- Learn why the party is tracing a particular nimblewright.
- Determine whether the Stone, Neverember's hoard, or the Grand Game is involved.
- Appear generous, amused, and socially harmless.
- Quietly review the culprit's record of witness after the meeting.

Consequences:

- **Ask Zord:** Fastest route to the Gralhunds, but Jarlaxle enters the Grand Game with good intelligence on the party.
- **Steal the sales ledger:** The ledger is aboard the Eyecatcher. This avoids deliberately briefing Zord but requires a heist and leaves multiple owners to eliminate.
- **Find the crystal ball:** Records of witness are aboard the Scarlet Marpenoth and can directly show Gralhund control. This is a larger heist and future-session material.

If the party reaches Zord tonight, a strong ending is his smile fading for half a heartbeat when they describe the beard, followed by: "Yes. I believe I know exactly which household purchased your man."

## Backup Route: Backtracking Dalakhar

Investigation through taverns and messengers traces Dalakhar to the Inn of the Dripping Dagger in the Trades Ward. A DC 13 Investigation check finds it quickly; failure costs several hours but still finds it.

Dalakhar rented a room for one night and left that morning.

- The room was professionally scrubbed.
- Investigation DC 12 finds ash in the lamp from Renaer's burned meeting reply.
- A black stone disk painted with Xanathar's beholder sigil lies openly on the pillow: a death mark.
- Four days after Dalakhar's death, Kalain's letter can arrive if the party leaves contact information. This is future depth, not tonight's core clue.

### Xanathar Response Team

The room is watched by Gorath's team of four duergar. They prefer to surround, question, and intimidate the party about Dalakhar rather than begin a public fight.

- Same day: they do not yet know Dalakhar is dead and demand to know where the gnome went.
- After one day: they know he died and can reveal that Urstul Floxin was following him.
- If violence starts, one duergar blocks the stairs, two enlarge, and one tries to capture a talkative PC.
- If defeated or searched, the team can carry N'arl Xibrindas's directive to obtain better equipment from Grinda Garloth, plus one healing potion. This turns the encounter into a future clue rather than a detached fight.

For a faster or less lethal version, keep one duergar outside as lookout and have the remaining three withdraw after taking serious damage.

## Cassalanter Plea Trigger

The Session 14 tube named a mysterious "Stone," but the party does not yet know the full identity or function of the Stone of Golorr. Recommended ruling: the vague tube reference does **not** fire the plea by itself.

Trigger the Cassalanter approach when any one occurs:

- The party learns the name or purpose of the Stone of Golorr.
- The party learns the Grand Game is a hunt for Neverember's hoard.
- The party learns the trail points to Gralhund Villa.

Delivery can be a Willifort calling card at Trollskull or a discreet invitation handed over as the party leaves an investigation node. The audience itself can wait until next session unless the party goes immediately.

If they do go now, the Cassalanters let them meet Elzerina and Terenzio, claim Asmodean cultists cursed the twins at birth, and say a ritual costing one shy of a million gold coins can save them. They ask for help recovering Neverember's hoard and conceal the required deaths of ninety-nine people.

## Trollskull Consequences

Do not settle business costs during the rescue. After the Watch scene, present choices:

- Close for mourning and repairs, or operate as a neighborhood relief kitchen.
- Spend from the 637 gp loan reserve, seek guild credit, or ask patrons for aid.
- Decide who protects Corellon's Crown and Fala's research/property.

Working accounting if needed: 50 gp and two days for glass, doors, and street-facing repairs. Running a relief kitchen costs another 10 gp but prevents a Fireball penalty on the first tenday business roll. Abandoning the damaged tavern without delegating care gives `-10` to that roll. Record any result only after the players choose.

|---|---:|---|

| Crime-scene freedom | 25-35 min | Urstul leaves; secure witness clues and Dalakhar evidence. |
| Watch and Renaer | 25-35 min | Establish Dalakhar and narrow the attack to a nimblewright. |
| Research and first owner | 45-60 min | Reveal Zord as common seller. |
| Final branch | Remaining time | End on Davin, Zord, Dripping Dagger pressure, or the Gralhund name. |

## Flexible Endings

- **Emotional:** Fala's covered body is carried into Corellon's Crown while Lif writes their name once on the broken chalkboard.
- **Mystery:** Renaer identifies Dalakhar and realizes the tube's gnome was coming to them.
- **Personal:** The door of the Crommor villa opens and Davy sees her father's scowling construct in the hall.
- **Threat:** Duergar close the Dripping Dagger room door behind the party.
- **Grand Game:** Zord names the Gralhunds.
