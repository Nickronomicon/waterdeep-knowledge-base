# Session 19 - Quick Run Sheet

**Read this first.** Three hours, voice, Discord. Resume **Ches 23 at the fair's pier**. Goal: get the Fireball nimblewright's buyer from the ledger and get away. [[Prep/Session Prep - 2026-11-12]] has full scene cards, rules, source distinctions, and contingencies. Everything new here is proposed prep, not played fact.

## First Ten Minutes

- Post [[Prep/Session 19 Handouts/01 - Recap]] and [[Prep/Session 19 Handouts/02 - Known Ship Facts]]. No month has passed in-world.
- Confirm actual HP/spells: theater fight followed the last long rest; Broethuul was revived with 9 HP, Elior intoxicated. Do not silently refresh them.
- Five breathing potions are documented, six were tentatively recalled. Agree count/duration before use. Zelifarn can surface to talk, saving a potion.
- Ask: “Dragon, one piece of research, or straight to the ship?”

## Three-Hour Pace

**0:00 recap → 0:15 dragon/research → 0:40 plan → 0:55 approach → 1:45 office → 2:15 escape/bargain → 2:50 debrief.**

If planning circles, ask each player for one job. Post [[Prep/Session 19 Handouts/03 - Plan And Preparation]]. Get entry, reader, support/distraction, exit, and **one prep skill/tool/ability per PC**. Explain flashback work when it matters; resources/costs still apply. No exhaustive survey required.

**Opportunity:** after they spend time watching/preparing, visibly row Zord ashore on business. He expects roughly an hour away. If they act before this departure, he is still aboard. Don't relocate him based on their route.

## Zelifarn In Five Minutes

Friendly, curious, remembers them. Offer **one** useful favor for an honest commitment to discuss helping him later: tow their boat/pickup **or** surface conspicuously to distract bow watcher + mate for **three exploration beats**. Stern guard stays. Agree signal and pickup. Persuasion DC 14 for both favors; failure still gets one. Transit from diversion to pickup takes a beat.

**Remix grievance if asked what he wants:** Jarlaxle killed his mother Asphosis and took her crystal ball, now in the hidden vessel under Eyecatcher. He wants it back. He does not know its full function. If asked who Zord is, the recommended version lets him identify Jarlaxle; that's a new reveal, not recap knowledge. Offer the crystal as a later score; don't require it before the ledger. No free shapechanging, breathing, or ship-sinking dragon attack.

Without the dragon, launch cover, a distraction, or a stern climb all work.

## Ship, Guards, And Choices

**Use the cabin they already visited.** Move the published J30 ledger/ward functions into the table-established aftcastle office; don't send them to a different room.

**Water → deck → stern landing → office.** Deck also reaches service passage → wardrobe → office panel. Rear office window opens toward water. Bow-to-stern 60 feet; landing-to-office 15; office 20 across. These are proposed staging dimensions.

Four exterior watch: **W1 bow, W2 amidships, W3 stern, M1 mate circuit every third beat**. Others below. Beat = significant exploration task (~minute); use rounds once initiative starts. Fix positions; NPCs react to evidence.

- **Board normally:** coherent supply/visitor cover Deception DC 13, otherwise 15. Crew know each other; named impersonation needs support.
- **Stern/window:** distract/evade W3, rope climb automatic unless under pressure; exposed approach Stealth DC 13. Window latch tools DC 13, or break noisily. Window is 10 feet above water; reveal on close reconnaissance.
- **Office door:** lock tools DC 15. Small face/script visibly warns of magic on inspection; Arcana DC 13 or detection identifies it. Touching exterior handle triggers spoken alarm, **even with a key**. Window/panel avoids it; applicable magic/prep can solve it.
- **Wardrobe:** service passage access, outer lock DC 15; deliberate search finds connecting panel, Investigation DC 13 finds it quickly. Unwarded alternate entry.
- **Attendant:** silent nimblewright guards **private floor hatch**, not paperwork. Approaching hatch gets a warning/interposition; insist or attack and it fights. Don't make a desk search trigger combat automatically.

## Pressure, Book, Exit

Draw **Alert 0 / 1 / 2 / 3**. Suspicious noise → question → search → bell/pursuit. Advance for actual exposure, not elapsed planning. Door ward sets at least 2. At 3, **two extra crew arrive after two announced beats**. Never teleport the whole crew into the office.

**Reach the desk = find the core clue.** Genuine Common-language record, no last Investigation gate. Read/jot key detail: one beat; copy entry: two; copy whole sales section: ten. Take book immediately if needed, leaving obvious evidence. In combat use an action to locate the illustrated entry and another to jot name/address.

Then post [[Prep/Session 19 Handouts/05 - Sales Entry]]: **House Gralhund, Saerdoun Street, North Ward**, matching beard/hat/red cape. Purchase evidence, not proof of murder or current Stone custody. No full buyer-count assertion.

Clean exit: let it work. Pursued exit: resolve **off ship**, then **reach pickup/break pursuit**. Failure costs time, exposed identity, chosen gear, separation, or a blocked route; not automatic group capture. Tap stones: 60 feet, audible 10 feet. In initiative use actual movement/ranges, state spell targets before commitment. Use [[Prep/Session 19 Handouts/04 - Ship Positions]] and [[Prep/Session 19 Handouts/06 - Extraction]] as player-filled reminders.

## If They Fight Or Get Caught

Optional **homebrew crew profiles**, replacing printed drow combat stats tonight:

- **Crew:** AC 13, HP 11, speed 30; one attack +4, cutlass 1d6+2 (5) or crossbow 1d8+2 (6), range 80/320; Athletics/Perception +2, passive 12.
- **Mate:** AC 15, HP 27, speed 30; two cutlass attacks +5, 1d6+3 (6); Athletics/Perception +3, passive 13.
- Other checks/saves: crew Str/Dex +2, mate Str/Dex +3, otherwise +0. Both are drow under human illusions; darkvision 120, charm-save advantage, immune to magical sleep; sunlight hinders attacks/sight checks. Real physical evidence can expose the disguise. Use two crew initially; mate only if actually present. Crew seek capture with melee, not lethal finishing blows.
- **Attendant if provoked:** AC 18, HP 45, speed 60; three +6 attacks, damage 8/8/6; resists nonmagical weapons; advantage on magic saves; reaction +2 AC vs one melee. Full condition/save details in main prep. Dangerous for this party: escape/parley, not mandatory kill.

Zord is a formidable duelist (published AC 24, HP 123), not a fair level-3 boss. If intercepted, crew offer surrender; actual capture can lead to bargaining. He isn't automatically at their escape route and doesn't know what nobody reported.

**Bargain:** truthful Dalakhar/Stone account and why the records matter → buyer information and safe departure. Accept counteroffers; no forced oath. If the party already escaped with the clue, don't force a meeting afterward. A clean win stays a win.

End with the Gralhund lead; if early, reconnaissance at the villa gate. Keep the crystal/submarine for the next score unless players actively choose it. No second unrelated ambush needed.
