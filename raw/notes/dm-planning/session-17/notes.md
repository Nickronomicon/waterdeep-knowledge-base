# Before we start play.
I want to go over heist structure, as the players may or may not be attempting one soon. I'm mostly following the heist structure in the remix, with a small twist. Here are the phases:

1. **Identify the score**: This step can be subtle, as players might not know that they are currently in the first phase of a heist. For example: The Identifying what/where is the Stone of Golorr has already begun. Once the party recognizes a tangible goal for a heist, we move th phase 2.
2. **Gather Information**: This portion is "louder" in terms of drawing faction attention. Player's attempt to learn about the locations and persons involved in the heist. This can be done through questioning people, pulling plans from city archives, or calling in faction favors. The end result is information about the location which will provide the players with a floorplan and possibly an event schedule. The floorplan will be a scene in Foundry where players can view and markup their heist plans.
3. **Onsite Surveillance**: Even louder than the last step. They physically (or magically) surveil the location ahead of time. This will expand their knowledge and may update their floorplans, allowing them to update their heist plan accordingly. Surveillance opportunities are listed in the Remix.
4. **Prep Work**: This phase deviates from the Remix's structure. Instead of telling the DM what they're doing to prep ahead of time, I'll ask that each player name a skill (or attribute) that they are using for their prep. The actual prep description and any skill checks are deferred to the operation. This will need the most explanation for the players.
5. **The Operation**: PCs carry out their plan. When a complication arises (or whenever a PC deems necessary), they can retroactively describe what they did to prepare for this heist and roll their previously declared skill to attempt to surmount the obstacle. This roll will be taken with advantage (I won't tell the players that it's an advantage roll ahead of time, I'll make it sound like a reward for a good description)

Once that's out of the way, we'll continue where we left off.

# A Request from Victoro Cassalanter
The Skullduggers are contacted by Cassalanter, requesting they visit him at his villa as soon as possible. He's heard about the fireball and wants assurances that his investment is still sound. Upon arriving, they are instead greeted by Lady Ammalia. The scene plays out as written in Remix Part 1: Casalanters. Here is a quick list of revelations:
* The Cassalanter twins (Terenzio and Elzerina) are doomed on their 9th birthday (10 days after Founders' Day Tarsahk 11th).
* Enough money can save them, but they need it by Leiruin Feast on Tarsahk 9th.
* Neveremeber hid 500k embezzled gold dragons somewhere in the city (Players should have heard this)
* The Stone of Golorr is rumored to be the key to this treasure.
* They were following a gnome named Dalakhar on the suspicion that he had the stone on his person.

She does not mention the eye in her posession until the party arrives with the stone.

Elior's Scar: there's a low baseline heat for the entire visit to the village, but nothing Lady Ammalia says causes his scar to flare up. There's nothing about a mother protecting her children that goes against the natural order.
