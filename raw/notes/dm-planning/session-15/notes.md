Mid-morning on Ches 22. The party was just knocked to the ground by a loud explosion outside. They do not yet know that a nimblewright from the Gralhunds just assasinated Dalakhar and stole the Stone of Golorr. Outside the manor, there are many injured and dead. The most crucial:
1. Fala Lefaliir is dead. Their body is trapped under rubble and Talley Fellbranch + apprentices are desparately trying to free them, unaware of their fate.
2. Squiddly is trapped beneath burning debris and not responding. Nat and Jenks are desparately trying to pull him from the rubble and will plead with the party for help. Squiddly will die if not rescued.

## Immediate aftermath
Once the scene is mostly stable, the players will have an opportunity to question people. This is their chance to speak with Urstul Floxin, as he'll disappear before the watch arrives. This is also the party's best opportunity to search Dalakhar's body, but it will require a stealth/sleight-of-hand check to not be scene pilfering a corpse. Plenty of witnesses will point them towards searching for a nimblewright.

## The Watch arrives
The Watch takes over the scene (not Staget this time, see Dragon Heist, chapter 3). Player's can still question witnesses, but more overt investigation will be curtailed by the Watch. Ranaer arrives shortly after the Watch, and explains about Dalakhar (See Remix, Part 5C)

## The investigation.
Ideally, the trail works something like this:
Learn the attacker was a nimblewright -> Learn of a few owners of nimblewrights -> Learn that all the nimblewrights come from Zardoz Zord (Jarlaxle) -> Get the names of owners via questioning Zord or a heist of the Sea Maiden's Faire -> Find the Gralhunds.

I will mostly run this as written in Remix, part 5C, but I will make one minor change: Instead of Lord Labdar Adarbrent, one of the first revealed owners of nimblewrights (before pointing at Zord) will be Davin Crommor. The nimblewright description remains the same, but it will be fun to see what Davy does with this.

As soon as the players either a)uncover the existence of the Stone of Golorr, or b) learn that the grand game is a hunt for Neverember's hoard, or c) discover that the trail leads to Gralhund Villa, then Lady Cassalanter will approach the party for help in saving her children (See Remix, Part 1: Cassalenters - A plea for help)  I don't expect this to happen during session 15, but I will be ready.

I don't expect combat in this session, but if the party spends their time backtracking Dalakhar (See Remix, Part 5d), then I will have a Xanathar Response Team encounter at the Inn of the Dripping Dagger.
